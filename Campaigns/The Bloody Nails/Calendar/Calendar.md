## Introduction

A month-by-month record of the [[The Bloody Nails|Bloody Nails]]' travels, from the wreck of the slave ship on Valtorra's southern coast to the present day. Each page below is one month of the [[Calendar of Elandis|calendar]] as it was kept at the table, marked up day by day with where the party went and what they did.

> [!tip] Reading a calendar page
> Every month is twenty-eight days across four weeks of seven, and each day shows the phases of the two moons — [[Selûne|Sel]], the bright moon, and Sha, the dark. The red hand is the party's own track through the month. Click any calendar to open it at full size.

For the sessions themselves, see the campaign page for [[The Bloody Nails]].
