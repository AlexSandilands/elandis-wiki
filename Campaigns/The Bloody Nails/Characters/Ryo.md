Ryo is an elven ranger of [[The Bloody Nails]], a full-blooded elf in an age when his kind have all but vanished from [[Valtorra]].

## Description

A full-blooded elf who keeps his ears hidden and his presence light, moving carefully through a land that has little love for his kind.

## Personality

Watchful and self-reliant, more comfortable reading a treeline than a crowded room — soft-spoken, hard to rattle, and colder than he looks when a job calls for it.

## Role in the Party

The party's ranger and tracker, at home in the wild places the Bloody Nails pass through. In a fight he takes the high ground and the longbow, marking the most dangerous foe and putting an arrow through it before it can do harm; out of one, he scouts, tracks and slips into whatever role gets the party where they need to be — a merchant, an Imperial officer, whatever the moment demands.

## Backstory

How one of the last true elves of [[Valtorra]] came to be aboard a slave ship, and what he has forgotten of who he once was, is a story still unfolding — and his own to tell.
