Dondar is a dwarven wizard of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A dwarf who has turned from the forge to the arcane, never far from the spellbook he guards as closely as any blade.

## Personality

Studious and sharp, measured where others are rash, with a scholar's appetite for old lore — and a stake in the fight against the Empire that runs deeper than it first appears.

## Role in the Party

The party's wizard and arcane backbone. He fights at range, with magic missiles, scorching rays and well-placed firebolts, identifies whatever enchantments the party turns up, and is the one they look to for the history and meaning behind it. Quiet heroics are not beyond him either — he once dove into the sea on a potion of water-breathing to haul a drowning comrade back to the surface.

## Backstory

Dondar hails from the dwarven holds, and what the Empire's reach cost his people there is a grief he carries quietly — the details his own to tell.
