Dondar is a dwarven wizard of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A dwarf who has turned to the arcane, carrying his study with him through the group's travels.

## Personality

Studious and sharp, with a stake in the fight against the Empire that runs deeper than it first appears.

## Role in the Party

The party's wizard and arcane spellcaster.
