Vaeleran is a human fighter of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A human warrior in battered splint who stands at the front when the fighting starts — and, just as often, the one doing the talking when it doesn't, with a silver tongue for any guard, official or angry crowd standing between the party and where they need to be.

## Personality

Direct and dependable, the steel the party leans on — though a man haunted by a past he cannot account for, which surfaces at the worst moments in flashes of grey soldiers drilling in step and a pale hand closed around a violet light.

## Role in the Party

The party's fighter and, as often, its face. He holds the front line and puts himself between his companions and harm — once trading places with the rogue mid-battle to spare him a water elemental's grip — but it was as likely his words as his sword that carried the group through: a knack for persuasion and a hard-edged way with a threat that talked the Bloody Nails past suspicious guards, hostile officials and tight corners that would otherwise have meant blood. When his buried memories rise, though, the line between friend and foe can blur, and the others have learned to talk him down as often as they stand beside him.

## Backstory

Who Vaeleran was before the Bloody Nails — and why the Empire would know his face — is a story still his own to tell.
