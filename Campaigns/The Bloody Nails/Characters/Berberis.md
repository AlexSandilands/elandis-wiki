Berberis is a human druid of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A weathered, middle-aged human, his red hair and beard greying and woven through with living vines, clad in furs and bark-worn armour.

## Personality

Gruff and blunt to a fault, the militant edge of a gentler calling.

## Role in the Party

The party's druid, wielding the wild's own fury against the Empire's encroachment.
