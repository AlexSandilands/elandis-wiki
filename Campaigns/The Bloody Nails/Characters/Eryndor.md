Eryndor is a half-elven rogue of [[The Bloody Nails]], and the one who gave the group its name.

## Description

A half-elf who keeps to the margins, quick of hand and rarely staying anywhere long enough to be remembered.

## Personality

Resourceful and quietly defiant, the sort to find the angle others miss.

## Role in the Party

The party's rogue. It was Eryndor who tore a rusty nail from the hull of the slave ship that first brought the group together — the act that gave the Bloody Nails their name.
