Neros is a half-elven druid, one of the early companions of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A half-elf at home in the wild, as likely to meet a threat as a dire wolf or a scuttling spider as on two feet, his carved quarterstaff in hand.

## Personality

Quiet and grounded, gentle with animals and protective of his companions, attuned to the places the party passed through and to stirrings the others could not sense.

## Role in the Party

In the group's early days Neros was their druid — scout, beast and guardian by turns. He ranged ahead as a spider or a wolf, threw himself into the front line when the fighting was thickest, and more than once carried a wounded ally to safety on his back. After the fight at the coastal cave, a growing sense that his path lay elsewhere drew him away, and he parted from the Bloody Nails to follow his own druidic calling.

## Backstory

The visions that called Neros onward, and where his road has carried him since, remain his own to tell.
