Neros is a half-elven druid of [[The Bloody Nails]].

## Description

A half-elf at home in the wild, drawing on the strength of the natural world.

## Personality

Quiet and grounded, attuned to the places the party passes through.

## Role in the Party

The party's druid, shaping the natural world to the group's needs.
