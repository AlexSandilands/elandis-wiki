Kai is a half-elf cleric and druid of [[The Bloody Nails]], sworn to [[Selûne]], the Moon.

## Description

A half-elf marked by his faith: when his power rises, constellations kindle across his arms in pale silver light, the sign of the moon goddess who claimed him.

## Personality

Steady and devoted, quick to mercy and a source of conviction when the party needs one — though never so pious he won't bend a rule, or gallop through a locked-down city in the shape of an elk, to see the right thing done.

## Role in the Party

The party's cleric, tending the wounded and channelling [[Selûne]]'s light — with a touch of the druid's gift besides, as likely to meet a crisis as a charging elk or a watchful bat as on two feet. He keeps the others on their feet, calls down radiant fire on what threatens them, and leans on a word of command or a wash of moonlight to take a fight in hand before it turns. Faith, for Kai, is also a calling he carries outward — dedicating the devout, counselling the lost, and chasing the truth of the gods wherever it leads.

## Backstory

What drew Kai to the moon goddess, and the road that set him aboard the slave ship where the Bloody Nails were forged, is his own to tell.
