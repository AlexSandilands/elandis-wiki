## Introduction

The Bloody Nails are a band of seven strangers thrown together by fate — and by the oppressive hand of the [[Valtorran Empire]] — aboard a slave ship bound for the labour camps of [[Skyreach]]. When a storm wrecked the vessel off the coast of [[Valtorra]] in the year 2506, they escaped their cell and washed ashore as free folk. Ever since they have roamed the continent doing what they can to impede the Empire at every chance, even as each of them chases ends of their own.

> [!note] The Rusty Nail
> The group takes its name from [[Eryndor]], who tore a rusty nail from the hull of the slave ship to free his companions from their cells. That same nail was driven into the skull of a [[Hand of The Empire]] at [[Skyreach]] and left there when the great bridge came down. Eryndor has carried a nail ever since, and the mark of a rusty nail has since become the symbol of the growing rebellion against the Empire.

## Calendar

The campaign keeps time by the [[Calendar of Elandis]] — a year of twelve twenty-eight-day months, each day marked by the phases of the two moons. Every month the party has lived through is recorded on the campaign [[Calendar|calendar]], annotated day by day with where they went and what they did.

## The Story So Far

From a shipwreck on Valtorra's southern coast to the dawnless heart of the lightless Vale far to the north, the Bloody Nails have grown from escaped prisoners into a name the Empire has learned to fear. What began as survival became rebellion, and what looked like a war against an Empress has revealed itself as something far older and far darker.

### Part I — Chains and the Long Road (Sessions 1–7)

Shipwrecked while being shipped to the labour camps, the seven broke free of their cell with a stolen nail and washed ashore on Valtorra's coast. Guarding a merchant caravan to [[Darmouth]], they fell in with the rebellion against the Empire and took a mercenary contract that led them into a goblin-held temple in the hills.

### Part III — The Fall of Skyreach (Sessions 14–16)

Battling Imperial warships across open water, the Nails infiltrated the labour camp, freed its slaves and brought the colossal bridge crashing down — leaving their nail in a fallen [[Hand of The Empire]]. In the rubble, an enemy named [[Vaeleran]] "Korvas", and the first thread of his buried past came loose.

### Part IV — The Secrets of Val Miriel (Sessions 17–26)

The road north brought murder, a reunion with Eryndor's father [[Jarek]], and the capital of [[Val Miriel]], where the resistance reached into the governor's own house. An accidental spark lit a city-wide uprising; beneath a goldmine the party struck an unlikely pact with an ancient drider queen, and the moon goddess [[Selûne]] marked [[Kai]] as something more than a healer.

Hunted out of Val Miriel through the sewers, the Nails carved a path across the frozen north — settling a ranger's vendetta in [[Lighthaven]], freeing the [[Frostwardens]] from an Imperial camp (and earning their name in the doing), and braving remorhazes, yetis and avalanche along the [[Frostway]] to reach the besieged city of [[Val Noren]].

At Val Noren the [[Dread General Gerard Blackmarsh]] tore open Vaeleran's mind and woke the loyal Imperial general within. The party freed the captured [[King Marius Noren]] from a necromancer's crypt, cut down Blackmarsh, and — with the Mawbreakers at their back — broke the siege, liberating the Shield of the North.

### Part VII — Blood of the Silmara (Sessions 43–46)

A secret surfaced that changed everything: [[Ryo]] is the last heir of the elven royal line, and [[Eryndor]] his cousin. Carried by the archmage [[Ayana Syndrosa]] through a gateway into the [[Faewild]], they crossed its riddling courts to [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]], the Shining City — a refuge of the elves, a people thought lost for a century.

### Part VIII — The Vault and the Vale (Sessions 47–55)

Before the elven Confluence, Ryo's claim was tested in the Vault of Memories beneath the World Tree's petrified root, where a cursed dragon-sage revealed the true enemy: the Empress is a lich, and behind her stands [[Kharazoth the Crimson Shadow]], older than the Empire and beyond ordinary death. Armed with a desperate plan, the Nails crossed into the [[Vale of Eternal Night]] and were brought before its ruler in the dawnless city of [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]]. There the [[Mirileth|Star-Veiled Queen]] revealed herself as Ryo's own ancestor [[Ellesandra Silmara|Ellesandra]] — and named a terrible price for the soulstone they had come to claim. Entering her service, the Nails passed a strange night as guests of the Dawnless City, spent most of the Queen's patience with a disastrous shopping expedition in borrowed faces, and slipped back out to the Barrow-Ring to settle her first demand: the death of the crone [[Ymrageth]]. They paid it in full — descending into the barrow through a tunnel at its crown, cutting through the crone's last honeyed lies, and destroying her and her entire coven before stripping her den of its trophy-hoard. Then came the long hunt home: the den burned behind them as a beacon, the wilds of the Vale swallowed Ryo in a pit of false ground, and a band of the Hunt — armoured centaurs and a monstrous worg — ran them up a mountainside until they leapt clear on the [[Zephyr Cuffs]]. The Queen's own steeds carried them through the Wall of Night, and with them came her pale guard Evrath, who woke as [[Asha Vayne]], Kaelen's son, sixty years lost — and a small elven girl calling herself Ellessa, whom [[Grumble, The Anniahilator|Grumble]] let across the [[Liar's River]] only after a stare-down and a game of chess.

## Allies & Adversaries

### Friends of the Rebellion

- [[Barak Stormrider]] — captain of the Mawbreakers, the rebel fleet that harries the Empire at sea.
- [[King Marius Noren]], the Reclaimer — the rebel king freed by the Nails, now a rallying point for the north.
- [[Ayana Syndrosa]] — an elven archmage of seven centuries, patron to the party; brilliant and dangerous in equal measure.
- [[Illythia Voss]] — elven stateswoman of Sumara and Ayana's closest companion.
- [[Gemma Finegold]] — the merchant and rebel contact who first set the party on the road.
- [[Governor Tarlis Evandron|Governor Tarlis]] and [[Vallania Miriel]] — secret leaders of the resistance within Val Miriel.
- The [[Frostwardens]] — hardened rebels of the frozen north.
- [[Praetor'Varanous]] — an ancient dragon-sage imprisoned beneath the World Tree, keeper of the truth about the Empire's master.

### The Empire and Its Servants

- [[Empress Morganna Eventide]] — the century-old ruler of the Crimson Empire; in truth a lich sustained by a dark pact.
- [[Kharazoth the Crimson Shadow|Kharazoth]] — the ancient power the Empire ultimately serves, older than death itself.
- The [[Hand of The Empire|Hands of the Empire]] — the Empress's elite enforcers, who take their own lives before they will surrender.
- [[Dread General Gerard Blackmarsh]] — the warlord who besieged Val Noren and unlocked Vaeleran's buried self; slain by the Nails.
- [[General Korvas]] — the loyal Imperial general Vaeleran once was, woken within him and turned against his friends.
- The [[Red Priests]] — necromancer-priests who raise the Empire's dead and answer to a power beyond the throne.
- The [[Eye of the Empire]] — the Empire's web of spies and assassins.
