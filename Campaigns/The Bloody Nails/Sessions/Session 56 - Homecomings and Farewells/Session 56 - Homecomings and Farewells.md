## Summary

From the ridge above [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]], [[Asha Vayne]] let out a breath sixty years in the holding, pulled his hood low, and followed the party down through the city on curiosity alone. The ceremonial guards at the bridge let [[Ryo]] pass with barely a glance, and Asha whispered the question that would follow him all day: *"Who are you people?"* In a quiet room of the [[Royal Citadel]], while Asha sat with his boots on the table drinking water that [[Kai]] had charmed to taste like elven wine, Ryo found the girl Ellessa at the window and asked when she had last been here. *"A while,"* she said — and for a fraction of a moment it was not a child's face looking back at him. Then [[Ayana Syndrosa|Ayana]] arrived with [[Illythia Voss|Illythia]] and a scowling [[Kaelen Vayne|Kaelen]], whose only wish was for the outsider to leave — until the glass fell from his hand and his son said, *"Hi, Father."*

Kaelen crossed the room and held Asha as though he might vanish again. When he turned to Ryo, his anger gave way to something stranger: an apology, a hand on the shoulder, and a bow from the waist, as if some long-held wall in him had cracked. Behind wards raised against scrying, Ryo told Ayana and Illythia everything — of [[Mirileth|the Star-Veiled Queen]], of what she truly was, and that the child at the window was her vessel and wanted into the [[Vault of Memories]]. Ayana was aghast that they had brought her inside the walls, but [[Grumble, The Anniahilator|Grumble]] had let the girl over his bridge, and that alone stayed her hand. The girl herself was blunt: she would not do what she must until the party went down into the Hells and kept their end of the bargain. Illythia brought better news — the Confluence had agreed to act for [[Eirieneth Silmara|Eirieneth]], and the sealed record of the agents of the [[Emerald Ceremony]] would be opened to find someone in Val Luminor who could help free her.

The party scattered through the city on errands. At the Diminished Trove, [[Arcanist Levvel]] found a sleeping power in [[Eryndor]]'s spider amulet, put there by no arcane hand but by something greater from the deep places, and saw at once how the [[Emberblade]] had woken. [[Berberis]] sought out [[Warden Sylvaris]], who showed him how to splice his old, sentimental staff of flowers into the staff of the woodlands, and [[Dondar]] committed the teleportation sigil of the [[Locus Arcanum]] to memory. Then, in a vast hall within the [[Heartstone]], Ryo set his ring into the dais of the great [[World Gates|World Gate]] and opened it on the snowy farmlands of [[Val Noren]]. Ayana kissed Illythia goodbye — *"Keep in contact this time"* — and went invisible, leaving a trail of footprints in the snow and a last *"Thank you, Ryo."* Alone with the girl, the party settled their course: study the Hells, gear up, open the Vault, and go straight down after the archdevil's body before it can reform.

## Details

### Homecoming

* On the ridge above [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]], [[Asha Vayne|Asha]] raised his hood and planned to march straight into [[Palisade Eternal]] to surprise his father; [[Eryndor]] urged him to stay with the group and let them bring [[Kaelen Vayne|Kaelen]] to him.
* Ryo's persuasion (11) didn't win Asha's trust, but Asha's curiosity did — he rolled a 3 on his survival instincts and agreed to follow.
* [[Kai]] sent Message to [[Ayana Syndrosa|Ayana]] (first garbling "we have Kaelen" for "we have Asha"); her reply: Kaelen was looking quite grumpy last she saw him, but she'd see what she could do.
* Ellessa reached for [[Ryo]]'s hand; he refused, so with a small pout she took Eryndor's instead.
* The party came down through the city by the great stone elevators, through the commons and onto the Bridge of Recollection, where the ceremonial guards gave Ryo the barest glance and a nod, and let them all pass.
* Asha, head bowed: *"What on green earth was that? Why did they just let you pass? Who are you people?"*
* The stair spirals up around the [[Heartstone]] itself, passing a massive arched stone door partway up.

### The Citadel

* There is no throne room in the [[Royal Citadel]] — the only seat that marks a royal's station is the neutral place on the council at the Confluence.
* The party took a room with a long table, an eternal fire and great windows; Asha, stunned to be inside for the first time, demanded an explanation — *"From my perspective, you're a bunch of outsiders."*
* Kai used prestidigitation to make a cup of water taste like the Citadel's elven wine; Asha loved it, and Ryo, amused, poured him the real thing.
* Ryo checked his ring was still on his hand (it was) and went to Ellessa at the window, fist kept closed.
* **Mystery:** asked when she was last here, she said *"A while"* — and for a fraction of a moment Ryo was not looking into the face of a girl. Asked if she meant to destroy the city: *"Why would I do that? It's a beautiful city."*
* Ayana's second message: *"Finally convinced the old codger. We're coming down from the top."* Ryo went alone to meet them at the stair down from the council platform.

### The Old Commander

* Ayana led Kaelen and [[Illythia Voss|Illythia]] down; she hugged Ryo, and when he said he might be being scried upon, she exchanged a look with Illythia — *"she's the backup."*
* Kaelen: *"I think it's disgusting that they're letting you stay in here. Outsider."* Ryo promised him a present; Kaelen replied that the only present he wanted was for Ryo to leave.
* [[Berberis]] brought Kaelen a cup of the good wine while he waited, and the two talked militia structures. Kaelen laid out the city's defences: a ceremonial royal guard at the nexuses and Citadel, the [[Skyguard]] on griffins and wyverns, and the [[Palisade Eternal]] where martial training is done.

### Behind the Wards

* Ayana and Illythia walked a side room together, sending sigils into its corners, and locked hands to seal a ward against scrying.
* Ryo told them everything — [[Mirileth]], her two natures, and that Ellessa was some vessel or projection of hers — but deliberately never said the words *soul stone*, only "a valuable stone we need."
* **Reaction:** Ayana's jaw dropped at the truth of Mirileth's origins; she knew the Star-Veiled Queen well by reputation — *"You brought her here? What possessed you?"*
* Ayana kicked herself for never showing [[Dondar]] a teleportation sigil to bring them home.
* What eased her: [[Grumble, The Anniahilator|Grumble]] had let the girl across, and she'd be a fool to break a deal with him.
* Ayana was clear that opening the [[Vault of Memories]] is Ryo's decision alone, and no one knows whether its magic would recognise the girl's blood herself.

### Father and Son

* Ryo stepped aside at the door; Kaelen saw who was sitting in the chair and the glass fell from his hand. Asha: *"Hi, Father."*
* Asha, brittle under his arrogance: *"You really think that they could kill me? Why didn't you come look for me?"* — and Kaelen pulled him into a hug: *"I'm so sorry. I thought you were dead."*
* Kaelen rounded on Ryo — *"You playing games with my son?"* — then stopped, put a hand on his shoulder and said *"I'm sorry, Ryo."*
* **Character Moment:** Ryo's insight showed a barrier Kaelen had held for years starting to shatter — the outsider he'd seen as nothing, even a threat, now earning a glimpse of respect.
* Ryo: *"You have done a wonderful job protecting the city, and I will be relying on you again."* Kaelen bowed at the waist; Ayana raised an eyebrow and Illythia smirked.
* Asha apologised for his attitude on the way out — *"Don't be strangers."*
* Kai sent Asha a Message to get his memory checked at the Luminary Canton — he might be cursed. Concern crept onto his face.
* Berberis clapped Kaelen on the shoulder: *"Sometimes we need a bit of change to make things happen."* — *"I think you might be right."*
* Illythia chuckled at seeing Kaelen put in his place; Ayana nudged her — *"Maybe he's growing."*

### What Do You Know About Hell?

* Berberis's opening question, before returning to his wine. Ayana had never been, and called the place complicated.
* Ryo admitted the deal: they must go to the Nine Hells and kill someone. Ayana: *"Making deals with fae — honestly. You really are a bunch of children."* Berberis: *"We are blunt tools."* — *"At least you're very effective."*
* **Revelation:** there is a [[World Gates|World Gate]] at the base of the Heartstone; Ryo is the only one who can drive it, and pointed right it could take them to the Hells.
* Ayana needed to return to the Material Plane to make sure [[King Marius Noren|Marius]] isn't up to no good — ideally before the party went anywhere they might not come back from.
* **Ellessa's terms:** she would not come to the Hells. *"Remember why you must go to the Hells. There's no point in going until I do what I must do. And I will not be doing what I must do until you fulfill your end of the bargain."*
* Then, a child again, she asked if there were trees to climb. Eryndor took her exploring with a heavy sigh; Kai went too.
* She jumped on couches, climbed the tree in the Citadel's open courtyard, and ended up hanging from a chandelier while Eryndor's mage hand spun her faster and Kai changed the colour of the flames.

### The Council of Three

* Berberis: *"I have faced down things beyond death, and that terrifies me."* Ayana: *"I don't want to imagine what could happen if we make the wrong move here."*
* Ryo asked how strong Grumble is — *"How strong is the sun?"*
* Ayana speculated on what the girl wants in the Vault: something left behind, knowledge she needs — or a curse planted in the root to crumble the city a century from now.
* Berberis warned them, with a look, that they would not want to know too much about the stone; the Queen guards knowledge of it dangerously.
* Ayana reasoned Vault first: the Queen won't release the stone until she's been inside.
* **Lore — the elves are fae:** Sumara is their homeland, and [[Ithilmara]] was only their seat on the Material Plane. They didn't flee [[Valtorra]] — they withdrew home.
* **Major Beat — the Confluence:** Illythia revealed the Council had met on Eirieneth and agreed something must be done.
* The agents of the [[Emerald Ceremony]] were sent cloaked and hooded, anonymous even to one another, so that none could betray the rest under interrogation — Ayana among them.
* Illythia, as Meridian of the [[Locus Arcanum]], controls the only record of who they are; the Council has agreed to unlock it, and she hopes to find agents in Val Luminor who can help free [[Eirieneth Silmara|Eirieneth]].
* On full return: if Ryo made his case and won Kaelen over, the elves could return to the world in force — but there is no going back from it, and the Empire would know at once. Illythia favours a slower start.
* Ryo: the war in Valtorra has already begun — one city in open revolt, another organising the rest. Illythia: *"The question is when and how we start it."*
* The agents are a network across the cities — the party may have met elves on their travels without ever knowing it.
* **Loot:** the crone's crystal ball, the [[Crone's Eye]], is a scrying ball that casts scrying as often as its attuned owner likes; [[Ymrageth's Grimoire]] remains an unreadable spellbook bound in skin, a face on its cover and fingers clasping its pages; Ryo claimed the +1 short sword.
* Dondar finally attuned his wand.

### Errands: The Diminished Trove

* Eryndor took Ellessa to [[Arcanist Levvel]] at the Diminished Trove; Levvel raised an eyebrow at the child but asked no questions.
* **Mystery — [[Amulet of the Arachnid Queen]]:** on a 19, Levvel found more in it than was known — a dormant power, not arcane, imbued by some greater entity; its magic is from the deep places. She couldn't pierce it and told him to be careful.
* The [[Emberblade]] had visibly grown and woken since she last saw it. *"Still haven't changed your mind on selling it?"* — Eryndor promised she'd be top of the list.
* Ellessa dug into a help-yourself box of mislabelled, expired potions; Eryndor let her keep one vial. She hasn't drunk it — just holds it, with a cheeky smile.

### Errands: Kai's Shopping

* Kai learned the Fundament Canton is the city's architects and engineers — aqueducts, the enchantments of the floating structures — and that a stranger can't simply ask to see [[Master Artisan Fenian|Meridian Fenian]].
* **Lore:** Sumara is not a religious society — its faith is the [[World Tree]], with only small pockets who worship the cosmic gods. Holy water isn't sold.
* The tattoo parlour had no powdered silver — metal is complicated for the fae — but sold Kai a tattoo needle for five silver.
* At the Diminished Trove, Kai laid the crone's severed arms on the bench and asked to harvest the hand bones for augury, and whether the skin might make hand wraps. Eryndor mouthed his horror.
* Levvel: *"I was starting to wonder if a psychopath had just walked into my shop."* She allowed the tissue might hold latent energy.
* Kai ritual-cast gentle repose with two copper pieces to preserve the arms for ten days; Levvel took them to her back room — he can check in later.

### Errands: The Greensward

* Berberis found [[Warden Sylvaris]] in the fields by the tree he planted, which is holding its own; she checks on it daily.
* She'd heard nothing of the Vale yet. *"Anything that we can rub old Kaelen's face in?"* — Berberis told her Kaelen was in a great mood. *"You might as well be saying the sky is falling."*
* She didn't recognise the staff of the woodlands but felt it hum — *"It's growing. It's yearning."*
* **Character Moment:** Berberis showed her his old staff of flowers, a gift from someone important he can't bear to discard, and asked if the two could be grafted.
* Sylvaris judged them compatible, almost related: meditate with both for a few hours as he would cultivate a sapling, and when he feels a binding between them, turn the staff of the woodlands into a tree — they may splice together.
* Berberis invited her to the northern groves of Valtorra once the gates open — *"once we deal with the bloody undead."*

### Errands: The Locus Arcanum

* Illythia took Dondar to the [[Locus Arcanum]], where a minute's study committed its teleportation circle to memory.
* Ryo spent the time alone, turning over whether there was any way out of the Queen's deal — and concluding there wasn't.

### The Gate Beneath the Heartstone

* The group reconvened at the Citadel; Ayana had changed back into her black leathers, ponytail and half-moon glasses, her ears no longer elven.
* Down the spiral stair, Ayana opened the great arched door — stone leaves twenty or thirty feet high — onto a cavern like the halls of the Vault, ringed with faintly glowing rune-carved pillars.
* At its heart stood a [[World Gates|World Gate]] far grander than Ithilmara's — dozens, perhaps hundreds, could pass at once — with a small stone dais and a slot to fit Ryo's ring.
* Ayana suggested Ithilmara as the sure route, but Ryo chose to aim straight for [[Val Noren]] — the farmlands where he once spent eight hours channelling a spell.
* Kai granted guidance; Ryo rolled a 69 on the d100. The ring tugged at his essence, the runes whirled to life, and the stone split down its seam onto cold, snowy fields swaying in the wind — dead on target.
* **Farewell:** Ayana kissed Illythia — *"Keep in contact this time. I'll be expecting to hear from you."* She turned invisible; footprints appeared in the snow beyond, turned back, and her voice said *"Thank you, Ryo. It's okay. You can close it now."*
* Illythia wiped away a tear and raised her chin. *"We'll need you to be able to, so that we can send more."*
* Ellessa watched it all with fascination, still clutching her vial.
* Illythia then sketched a teleportation circle on the floor and vanished to the Locus Arcanum for a drink.

### The Decision

* The party worked through the logistics: teleportation circles can't cross planes. Dondar knows circles in Val Noren, Glimmerforge and now the Locus Arcanum — but the only way back to the Faewild is through Ithilmara, whose ward harms anyone not elven.
* Kai floated taking Ellessa to the Material Plane; the room was not convinced.
* Other threads were weighed and set aside for now: [[General Korvas|Korvas]] heading for the [[Darkwood]], Dondar's people, and the rescue of Eirieneth.
* **The plan:** research the Hells first (the Circle of the Scroll), gear up, open the Vault for the girl, then go straight through the Gate to the Hells — the archdevil's body must be destroyed before it reforms, and every hour after the stone is broken makes that fight worse.
* **Cliffhanger:** next stop, the [[Vault of Memories]] — with the Star-Veiled Queen's vessel at Ryo's side.
