## Summary

Before anything else, [[Ryo]] led [[Eryndor]] alone into the hall of the [[World Gates|World Gate]] beneath the [[Heartstone]] to settle a question that had been gnawing at him. Eryndor set the ring into the dais himself, thinking of the [[Whispering Woods]] where he and his father had spent so many days, and the gate stayed dark. Whatever the old magic answers to, it answers to Ryo alone. In the [[Lectorium]], the party drove the archivists hard for everything known of the Hells: nine layers, every traveller landing first in the war-torn waste of Avernus, the river Styx the fastest road down, gold welcome, souls minted into coin, and names to be given sparingly. Buried in an account of some hideous play staged for the great devils, they found Maloreth's name beside a theatre in the Rustward quarter of Dis. [[Kai]], digging alone, found that the [[Darkwood]] was once the seat of an ancient civilisation that fell in the [[Chaos Wars]], and remembered the out-of-place, rune-scarred [[Emberspire]].

The errands that followed were gentler. [[Illythia Voss|Illythia]], red-eyed after [[Ayana Syndrosa|Ayana]]'s departure, convinced Kai he was cursed and dying, made him gulp scalding tea, and then confessed the only curse was that he worried too much. She gave the party the first name from the opened ledger of the [[Emerald Ceremony]]: Theradin, an elf deep undercover in the upper ranks of Val Luminor as Tobin Ashe. She read [[Ymrageth's Grimoire]] as a spellbook of dream script and told [[Dondar]] he could learn it at an hour a day, no more. In the [[Royal Citadel]]'s courtyard, [[Berberis]] grew his staff into a tree around his old staff of flowers, and the two became one, a canopy blooming red, blue and violet. Ryo attuned the crone's crystal ball and turned it on [[General Korvas|Korvas]]: a red-cloaked rider leading imperial soldiers through a snowstorm on the northern coast road, not yet in the Darkwood. A second look found [[Vallania Miriel|Vallania]] stalking the alleys of a [[Val Miriel]] whose uprising had spread, and her answer to Kai's sending was grim: *"Father is playing both sides right now… He doesn't know. Hoping for reinforcement soon."*

That night Ryo went with Kai to the [[Palisade Eternal]], and for the first time spoke to [[Kaelen Vayne|Kaelen]] not as an outsider but as one of his people. Kaelen admitted his long creed of strength through withdrawal may have been the wrong path, and promised that if Ryo called for them as their leader, the elves would come. Ryo asked him to begin readying Sumara's forces quietly and to put a stealthy strike force for Val Miriel before the Council as a request, not an order. Kaelen smiled at that, and at Kai's sketches of spell-enchanted spears and ships that sail the sky, which, he pointed out, already fly over [[Seraphon]]. Come morning, the party gathered in the depths of the Royal Citadel before the dais of the [[Vault of Memories]], the girl Ellessa holding someone's hand, and waited for Ryo to open the door.

## Details

### The Ring Test

* While Kai taught Ellessa to brew joffy in the Citadel kitchens, Ryo slipped away with Eryndor to the [[World Gates|World Gate]] chamber, sending the girl with the others.
* Ryo offered Eryndor the ring: *"Why don't you see if you can do this?"* Eryndor: *"This better not kill me. The healer's not here."*
* Eryndor first floated the ring over with mage hand; above the dais the hand vanished and the ring clattered to the floor. Eryndor suspected an anti-magic field.
* Eryndor set the ring in the slot by hand, thinking of a place in the [[Whispering Woods]] near the bend where he and his father spent much of their time. The ring pulled itself into place.
* **Major Beat:** Riv paused the table before the d100 to stress that either result would mean something. The gate stayed dormant.
* Eryndor to Ryo: *"You are not allowed to die."* Ryo had tried it here rather than on the Vault, which he expects to be better guarded.

### Research in the Lectorium

* The party met at the [[Lectorium]], the Circle of the Scroll's great library in the Mnemorium Canton, where the familiar clerk helped them search; Kai cast enhance ability on the researchers and Ryo cast it on himself.
* Eryndor and Dondar led the investigation with 27 and 26.
* **The Hells:** nine layers, each more dangerous to mortals. Without great power or ties to a deeper layer, every traveller arrives in Avernus, the first layer and endless battlefield of the Blood War.
* Even the old Silmara could never open a World Gate deeper than Avernus, however well they knew the Hells.
* **Major Clue:** an obscure account of a hideous play attended by important devils, held in a theatre in the Rustward quarter of Dis, named Maloreth among them.
* Dis, the second layer, is one city spanning the whole layer.
* Devils slain in the Hells die for good; slain elsewhere, they reform.
* The Styx runs through every layer and is the fastest way across Avernus. Never enter the water; ferries run on it.
* **Currency:** material gold is welcome to devils. Soul coins hold a damned soul; break the coin and the soul is freed, usually to be devoured. Held close, the coins can almost be heard.
* **Names:** asked for your true name, give it sparingly. It doesn't bind you, but it grants power over you and makes you known.
* Berberis asked after the creatures: in Avernus, mindless swarms like dretches and lemures attack anything, and vulture-like beasts snatch prey to drop from a height.
* Devils are evil but lawful: they won't kill mortals on sight in Dis if they belong there, but will try to trap them in bad bargains. Demons will tear mortals apart just for being mortal.
* Dondar asked what alcohol the Hells serve. There probably isn't much.
* **Lore:** on his own search (Kai, 15), Kai found a scroll describing a vanished civilisation of grand temples where the [[Darkwood]] now grows, fallen in the [[Chaos Wars]], and connected it to the [[Emberspire]] and its anti-magic runes.

### Errands

* Ryo collected the [[Zephyr Cuffs]] and returned them to the [[Skyguard]].
* Kai, Dondar and Eryndor went to [[Illythia Voss|Illythia]] at the [[Locus Arcanum]], docked in the upper rims of the Aetherian Canton; Eryndor turned back to take Ellessa home.
* Illythia's eyes were still faintly red. On Ayana: *"Tough to say goodbye. But there's hope now."* And: *"We know our duty, and the duty is to the city."*
* **Character Moment:** Kai mentioned the hag arms he'd left with [[Arcanist Levvel]] and asked her to check him for curses. She touched his forehead, recoiled in horror, sat him down and had him drink scalding tea (4 fire damage).
* Illythia: *"I believe you are cursed with stressing too much. Do you really think I would let you walk in here fully cursed and not tell you?"*
* **Major Beat, the Confluence:** after long debate, the Council was swayed by signs of the Worldbreaker's return, the rising human rebellion, and a member of the royal family held by the Empress, and voted to open the ledger.
* **Lead:** the first name is Theradin, an elf deep undercover in the central ranks of Val Luminor society, going by Tobin Ashe.
* Illythia advised against contacting him by magic in case it's watched. As a last resort she knows him and could send a message herself, and he might be scryed if unprotected.
* Dondar asked for an operative in the dwarven cities. Names can't be handed out freely, as every name revealed endangers the others. A request with a clear purpose could go to the Council.
* Illythia admired the crystal ball and hoped to have it. She advised them to keep it, protect it, and keep it from the wrong hands.
* **[[Ymrageth's Grimoire]]:** Kai dropped it in fright. Illythia opened it with a click of her fingers to swirling text, and its real fingers clenched and relaxed. *"They certainly have some adorable habits over there in the Vale."*
* She judged it a spellbook in difficult dream script, likely full of powerful, nasty spells. She can teach Dondar to read it, but only an hour a day, or it brings restless nightmares. Dangerous, but not cursed.
* Leaving, Dondar asked whether Kai was attracted to Illythia. Kai: *"Hell no. She's got Ayana."* Dondar was only trying to bond.

### The Flowering Staff

* In the Citadel courtyard, Berberis planted the staff of the woodlands beside his old staff of flowers, used speak with plants, spent the flower staff's charges, and urged the growing tree to take the flowers in.
* **Reward Obtained:** on a DC 17 Nature check, branches curled around the flower staff and drew it into the trunk. Flowers burst from the bark and the canopy filled with red, blue and violet blooms.
* Dismissed, the two staves are one, woven into each other. Berberis will name it later.

### Visions in the Crystal Ball

* Back at the Citadel, Kai suggested shopping for disguises. Ryo: *"We're probably just out of place no matter where we go."* Riv: senior devils likely speak Common, many others won't.
* Eryndor proposed using the crystal ball on [[General Korvas|Korvas]].
* **Reward Obtained:** Ryo attuned the crystal ball, the [[Crone's Eye]], over a short rest. It scrys freely, at DC 17.
* **Korvas:** through snow, a hooded rider in a long red cloak, a huge sword over his shoulder, leading imperial soldiers on a road. Ryo's survival check placed them on the northern coast road of [[Valtorra]], not in the Darkwood and not on the Frostway.
* Eryndor checked the sending stones. [[Governor Tarlis Evandron|Tarlis Miriel]]'s terse reply: *"Good to hear from you. Excellent news. When are they giving us support? Things are getting dire out here."*
* Kai sent a sending to [[Alyn the First]]: *"Disciple Alyn, never lose faith, seek Selûne's guidance. A gift is buried beneath the Silver Oak in St. Aveline's."* Her reply: *"Most Holy One… my faith neither wavers. Many are beginning to learn and follow."*
* Kai had buried a hundred platinum a foot down among the oak's roots, with [[Selûne]]'s sigil carved into the bark.
* **[[Val Miriel]]:** Ryo scryed [[Vallania Miriel|Vallania]]. The city glowed with embers, rubble barricades had spread beyond the southern quarter, and the Military Way was lit and fortified. He found her hooded with a bow on her back, sliding up an alley at midnight.
* Kai's sending startled her into the shadows. Her reply: *"Kai, you startled me. Things are tense in the city… Father is playing both sides right now. I'm trying to do what I can. He doesn't know. Hoping for reinforcement soon."* Ryo's insight said she was honest.
* Riv's reminder: Tarlis is working with [[General Varan Brado|General Brado]] to keep the city in hand while secretly starting the rebellion, so he's unpopular on both sides. The [[Mawbreakers]] are en route.
* Kai's second message promised as much help as they can send; she answered *"Please hurry."*
* The party settled it: the Vault in the morning. Berberis noted the Queen can't cross them too badly, because then they simply won't kill the devil.

### Council with Kaelen

* Ryo asked Kai to send to [[Kaelen Vayne|Kaelen]], cast in Druidic so Ryo couldn't hear Kai call him *"his Highness."* Kaelen: *"I'm at the Palisade Eternal catching up with my son. What is needed so urgently?"*
* Ryo's second message: a war is coming that can't be avoided, and he needs to know the city's strength. Kaelen: *"If a meeting is required tonight, I'll make it known to the guards out front… Come."*
* Ryo went with only Kai, refusing to bring Mirileth's vessel, and with one guard escort through the sleeping tiers.
* At the [[Palisade Eternal]], a squat black fortress with a great sword carved down its face, Skyguard aviaries above, Ryo found Kaelen with [[Asha Vayne|Asha]], who stepped out to give them time.
* **Character Moment:** Ryo: *"There is a war coming that will spill across all the planes."* Kaelen: strength in numbers, strength in home, leave the material realm behind. *"But… I see now that may have been the wrong path."*
* Ryo admitted he has only known who he is for about a week, alone all his life until he met the party two months ago. Kaelen called his "outsider" judgement hasty and unfair, and apologised again.
* **Sumara's strength:** soldiers and mages of the Circles of the Blade and the Weave, the Skyguard's elite aerial assault, and some less public members of Illythia's circle.
* The Council's view is the same: they must eventually strike back into the Material Plane, but going now would alert their enemies. Ryo's judgement of when may decide it.
* **Major Beat:** *"I assure you, if you call for us, if you call for us as our leader, we will come."*
* Ryo laid out the war: two cities in open revolt, one that has beaten an imperial army, and a governor playing double agent. Kaelen: *"All we need is other cities to start falling, cut off the Empire's supply lines… surround them, wash them away."*
* On the dwarves: a *"bubbling cauldron ready to burst,"* and a friend of the dwarves in the party would be a good, risky play.
* Kaelen picked up on Ryo's "we" and liked it. He'll begin readying forces quietly, with no announcements and no panic.
* Ryo asked for a stealthy strike force to help Val Miriel, coordinated with the agent already there through the unsealed ledger. Asked whether it should go to the Council: *"Then yes, please take my request to the Council. But it is a request, not an order."* Kaelen smiled.
* **Kai's Sketches:** drawn with his illuminator's tattoo, a spear that casts magic missiles. Kaelen knows spell-enchanted weapons and called the cost prohibitive, but would gladly accept any arsenal Kai brings back.
* Second sketch: ships that sail the sky. Kaelen chuckled that skyships are well known on [[Seraphon]], the continent far to the northeast, where expeditions sail into the wastes of [[Aetheris]] for relics.
* Kaelen to Ryo at parting: *"Please stay safe."*
* Kai's last sending of the night, to Vallania: *"Help is coming. Stay safe."* No reply came, but it arrived.

### At the Door

* The party took a long rest in the Citadel.
* **Cliffhanger:** in the morning they gathered in the lower chambers of the Royal Citadel, the little girl led by the hand, before the dais of the [[Vault of Memories]], waiting for Ryo to open it.
