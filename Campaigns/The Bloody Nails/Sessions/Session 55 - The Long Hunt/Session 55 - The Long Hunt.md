## Summary

The crone was dead, but her den still stank of what she had done there, and the party did not mean to leave it standing. [[Kai]] held out the hag's long, clawed arms while [[Ryo]] took them off at the elbow for the bones — and the moment they parted from her, the headless body of [[Ymrageth]] smoked, crumbled, and blew away as dust. Rocks rattled down from the hole they had entered by; [[Berberis]]'s owl, sent up to look, caught a glimpse of something eight-legged skittering away into the barrow-ring. Their killing was witnessed. [[Dondar]] loosed a dozen bolts of fire into the wooden platforms, oil was thrown, Berberis fanned the flames with a druid's wind, and the party fled the mound as it bloomed black smoke into the eternal night — a beacon, and behind it the first howls from deep within [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]].

They ran for the wilds, away from the road and the river, up through the crater wall and into the bones of some ancient, swept-away civilisation — fallen walls, and a giant stone head half-buried in the earth. There the dark swallowed Ryo whole: a patch of ground that was not ground drew him under to the neck, then past it, and every hand that reached for him only sank alongside. Berberis slammed his crooked staff of the woodlands into the pit and spent its power to raise a sixty-foot willow from it, and by its roots Ryo clawed his way up with his lungs burning. Then the Hunt found their scent — a line of riders far back on the road turning off it, a single great howl drawing nearer. What followed was a race up the mountain: Berberis seeded a chokepoint with a cordon of enchanted arrows, Ryo choked the slope with overgrowth, Kai lit a withered fungus to mask their smell and was engulfed in a cloud of necrotic spores for his trouble, and at the last Dondar painted the whole mountainside with an illusion of quicksand pits so convincing the pursuers looped and doubled back among them. From a cliff above the valley the party looked down at half a dozen armoured centaurs and a worg bigger than any of them, then leapt into the air on the [[Zephyr Cuffs]] with arrows falling short behind them and the beast howling from the edge.

A day's distance bought, they rested beneath a hidden hut in the jungle — and on the late watch [[Eryndor]] found the [[Emberblade]] answering his hand with spirals of golden light, more sword than he had known it to be. Hooves came out of the north: seven black steeds with manes of smouldering ember, ridden only by [[Asha Vayne|Evrath]], the pale soldier of the Citadel, with a small dark-haired elven girl asleep against his back. He had been sent by his lady to see them to the wall, he said, and lay down to sleep — and would not wake, until a hard shake brought him up with his sword out, demanding to know where his crew was. The man who woke was [[Asha Vayne]], son of Kaelen, and his last memory was a skirmish on the border sixty years gone. Ryo showed him the ring and told him whose son he was, and the fire-maned steeds bore them all through the Wall of Night into the light. At the [[Liar's River]], [[Grumble, The Anniahilator|Grumble]] welcomed Asha home, then raised a hand against the girl who called herself Ellessa; the two locked eyes until he offered her a game, and she beat him at chess two matches to one. Berberis lost his own game to the troll and paid with the whole story of the Vale. Grumble's verdict on the child: *"She is a threat. But she is not. We made an agreement. She knows the consequences."* The session ended on the road up to [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]], Asha's black Citadel armour stowed away in the bag of holding before the gates.

## Details

### Burning the Den

* The session opened in [[Ymrageth]]'s den after a short rest, the party debating how to destroy the place; the cavern was too large and solid to collapse.
* [[Kai]] held out the dead crone's arms while [[Ryo]] severed them at the elbow — Kai wants the hag's bones as material components for augury.
* **Mystery:** the moment the arms came free, the headless body, robes and all, disintegrated into dust and blew away — but the severed arms remained, and went into the bag of holding.
* Rocks tumbled from one of the openings in the ceiling; [[Berberis]] sent his owl up and it glimpsed an eight-legged creature skittering down the barrow and away into the barrow-ring.
* Kai checked the cavern's mushrooms for the explosive kind — these fae fungi were nothing like the ones from the material plane.
* [[Dondar]] fired a dozen fire bolts into the wooden platforms, two flasks of oil from the bag of holding were thrown on, and Berberis fanned the blaze with gusts of wind until it was an inferno too hot to stand near.
* Black smoke poured out of every crack in the mound — a beacon for anyone watching — and wolf howls answered from deep inside [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]].
* The party agreed they were unknown outsiders who had just killed a high-ranking figure of the Vale, and chose the wilderness over the road, and against a straight line toward [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]].

### Ruins in the Dark

* Berberis burned a charge of his staff of the woodlands for pass without trace; Ryo cast longstrider on Dondar to keep his short legs up with the group.
* Climbing out of the crater and into the wilds, the party passed the remains of an ancient civilisation — collapsed buildings, long tumbled walls, and a giant stone head some twenty feet tall, half sunk in the earth.
* Ryo, navigating, stepped into a patch of darker ground and sank to his waist, restrained — a quicksand-like pit.
* Dondar flew over and grabbed his pack straps; Kai used Steps of the Night to fly and pull too; Berberis latched onto Kai with thorn whip; [[Eryndor]] grabbed Berberis — and Ryo kept sinking until his head went under, holding his breath.
* Berberis's grey bag of tricks produced a weasel, which helped drag Eryndor's rope into the chain.
* **Character Moment:** Berberis stepped into the edge of the pit, slammed his staff of the woodlands down into it, and spent a charge to transform the staff into a sixty-foot willow, its roots tearing down through the pit to Ryo.
* Twelve feet under with things going cloudy, Ryo grabbed a root and, with Dondar and Kai heaving, hauled himself hand over hand to the surface.
* The rope of climbing got everyone clear; Berberis turned the tree back into a staff (roped, in case the pit swallowed it).

### The Long Hunt

* Berberis took the lead, spotted a patch of withered, black-splotched fungi and backed the party away as one puffed out a cloud of black dust.
* Through the owl, Berberis saw a line of fast-moving four-legged figures far back on the main road, searching — and a wolf howl; the pack left the road and turned toward the party.
* Kai tried to burn a fungus with prestidigitation to mask their scent — three burst in a 20-foot cloud of necrotic spores; he took 20 necrotic damage on a successful save, and it was loud.
* Kai dropped a huge cube of rain over the spore-field to muddle the smell, and the party ran uphill toward the mountains.
* **Chase sequence:** three successes needed before two failures.
* Berberis borrowed four arrows from Ryo, added the four he already had, and cast Cordon of Arrows at 4th level on a natural chokepoint — success.
* Ryo cast plant growth behind them, a hundred-foot radius of thorny roots and walls — success.
* Kai spent a spell slot to resummon his familiar as a crow, Pepper, with orders to learn the sound of the howl.
* Through Pepper's eyes the Hunt was less than a mile back and gaining; Kai's third-level fog cloud over rough terrain slowed them but not enough — first failure.
* **Character Moment:** Dondar cast hallucinatory terrain to smear the mountainside with illusory quicksand pits; on a natural 20 arcana the pursuers, trained to avoid such pits, looped and doubled back among the bluffs — third success.
* At the clifftop everyone charged the [[Zephyr Cuffs]] with 2nd-level slots.
* **The Hunt revealed:** half a dozen heavily armoured centaurs with long spears and bows, and an unnaturally huge, long-eared, dripping-fanged worg bigger than the centaurs — it had pushed straight through the overgrowth.
* The party jumped as the centaurs drew and fired; the arrows fell short, and the worg was left howling on the cliff edge. Pepper came alongside Kai, seeming to have learned the sound.
* They glided for hours, covering most of the twenty-five miles to the Vale's edge and buying roughly a day on the Hunt.

### The Hut in the Jungle

* Tired, the party chose to rest rather than risk exhaustion; Kai ritual-cast Leomund's tiny hut to hide them and seal in their scent.
* Kai lent Ryo the decanter of endless water to wash off the mud; watches were Ryo and Kai first, Eryndor and Berberis second.
* **Mystery — the [[Emberblade]]:** on watch, Eryndor drew the sword and found it humming and responding to his touch, spirals of golden energy running up the blade and around his hand; he has been given a new description of the weapon and, meditating on it, felt it awakening.
* Eryndor heard hooves approaching from the north; Berberis's owl found seven black steeds with glowing, ember-like manes and smouldering tails — nightmare steeds — carrying only one rider with a small figure behind.
* The rider was [[Asha Vayne|Evrath]], the pale-skinned Citadel soldier, with a sleepy, pale, dark-haired elven child — not one of the Vale's dark-skinned elves.
* The owl led Evrath to the clearing; Eryndor walked out of the hut to meet him.
* Evrath said his lady had told him to bring steeds, meet the party here, and see them through the wall; the child was "some child within the city" he was told to bring, apparently from the elven city — he knew nothing more.
* The Hunt had been heard causing a commotion but would not trouble a rider on nightmare steeds; they were "some ways back."
* Evrath pitched a small tent for the girl, ate his purple vegetables, and slept in the open; the party finished their long rest (Berberis's staff recharged fully).

### Asha Awakens

* In the morning Evrath would not rouse; Berberis "tapped" him awake for a d4 of improvised damage and he came up with his sword drawn — *"Who are you? Where's my crew? What have you done to them?"*
* **Major Reveal:** the man who woke was [[Asha Vayne]], son of [[Kaelen Vayne|Kaelen]], with no memory of the last sixty years.
* Berberis told him he had been under a long, sorcelled sleep, and pointed out the moon and the distant city.
* **Character Moment:** Ryo showed Asha the ring and named him — *"You're Asha Vayne, son of Kaelen, are you not?"* — and with a 16 persuasion Asha agreed to leave with them.
* The nightmare steeds lowered themselves to be mounted (a shorter one for Dondar) and tore through the terrain toward the wall at incredible speed.
* Ryo told Asha they were from Sumara, had met his father, that his party had been missing and presumed dead for decades — and that he himself was a son of the Silmara, born after the elves left the material plane.
* Asha's account: a skirmish on the border with the Hunt — his captain Iriak wanted to look through the wall to learn the terrain; a patrol on the far side took it as an incursion and the fight broke out before it could be de-escalated; the last he remembers is a blow to the head.
* Ryo confirmed no one from his crew ever came back; Asha fell silent.

### Through the Wall

* At the Wall of Night the steeds slowed; Berberis spoke with them and learned they would go no further for fear of outsiders, and that the party was free to return to the city.
* **Character Moment:** one steed poked its head through the wall to look at the outside, came back, met Berberis's eye and said *"I prefer the night"* before the herd rolled away.
* Passing through the wall was cold, thick, and tearing at the skin; on the far side the aurora sky and the distant [[Shimmering Peaks]] were a welcome sight.
* Asha and the girl both shielded their eyes from the light; she absent-mindedly took his hand, he pulled away, then relented and offered it back.
* The girl said her name was Ellessa, an orphan of the city who was playing outside, taken into the darkness, and kept in the city there "for a long time" until Asha said they were leaving.
* Asha offered to have his father find people to take her in.
* Asha recalled his crew playing games with the great birds of the plains — they can be scared off with a splash of fire — and the day's crossing passed without a feather in the sky.

### Grumble's Bridge

* The party reached the [[Liar's River]] to find [[Grumble, The Anniahilator|Grumble]] at his table in the middle of the bridge.
* Grumble greeted Asha by name — *"Welcome back, child... too long, too long"* — and hoped he would find the city to his liking.
* **Major Beat:** Grumble raised a hand against the girl; the two locked in a silent, tense stare until he asked, *"Would you like to play a game?"*
* Ellessa beat Grumble at chess in three lightning-fast games — Grumble took the first, she took the second and the third, pinning his king with a rook — and he nodded her through.
* Berberis stayed behind for a game of his own, staking a story against Grumble's assessment; Grumble took his king, and Berberis told him everything — the Vale, the crone, the bargains with the Queen.
* **Grumble on the girl:** *"She is a threat. But she is not... We made an agreement. She knows what is at stake. If she steps out of line — she knows the consequences."* Whether she tells the party more is up to her.
* Eryndor asked if the soul-taking was a joke; Grumble produced a rattling pouch from under his cloak, faint distant screams within, and invited him to play — Eryndor declined, "maybe next time."
* Across the bridge the mountains folded close as before; the girl's mood visibly brightened, looking about as though seeing the place for the first time in a very long while.
* **Cliffhanger:** before the gates of [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]] the party stowed Asha's black Citadel armour in the bag of holding and lent him a plain elven robe — the session ended on the road up to the city with Asha and the girl in tow.
