## Summary

At the threshold of the eternal night the party stepped through the whirling black veil and into the [[Vale of Eternal Night|Vale]], a beautiful, cold country of silver needle-trees, glowing fungus, and a river of pure night draining across the land beneath a black moon ringed in white fire. They resolved to walk in openly, as envoys of a kind, and [[Ryo]] alone kept his counsel — agreeing to hide that the blood of kings ran in his veins. The river itself proved its menace early: when [[Dondar]]'s newly-summoned bat dipped into the black water it came back grey and shivering, its very life stripped away by a curse. Following a carved road across the valley, the party heard the howls draw closer until the Hunt fell upon them — a dire warg the size of a horse and a ring of armoured centaurs, led by the grey, one-eyed warden [[Caradoc|Caradoc]]. Disarmed and encircled, they talked their way past his suspicion with a tale of trade and diplomacy from the Shining City, and were marched not toward the city gates but off the road, into the barrow-mounds beyond the wall.

There, in a webbed and cauldron-lit tomb beneath the earth, an ancient thing wearing the shape of a kindly old woman bound them in thorns and named herself [[Ymrageth]]. When [[Berberis]] let slip the words *soulstone anchor*, a flash of genuine shock crossed her face before the grandmotherly mask slid back into place. She spun a tale of a tyrant Queen preparing the Vale for war upon the Peaks, and offered them a bargain: carry a poisoned amulet into the royal audience and shatter it at the Queen's feet, and she would help them hunt down the stone they sought and let them leave the Vale alive. The party's insight pierced her sweetness — the crone was lying through her teeth — yet they took the deal, with Berberis choosing his words with great care, promising only to *see the amulet into the Queen's presence*. With [[Caradoc|Caradoc]] set to ensure they kept the bargain, they were led through the Antler Gates, up through the warrens and the muster-yards and the dark-elven court tiers, to the obsidian heart of [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]] and the doors of the Veiled Citadel.

The Star-Veiled Queen came as mist, coalescing into a figure ten feet tall — elven, but wrong, her gown shifting endlessly between black and white. [[Berberis]] presented the crone's amulet and exposed her treachery; the Queen dissolved it to dust with a wave of her hand and, with a thin smile, acknowledged how cleverly he had kept his word. But when the party again spoke of a *soulstone anchor*, all warmth drained from the hall. She crossed the room and killed [[Caradoc|Caradoc]] and his lieutenant where they stood — a hand thrust into a skull, a head wrenched backward — for no living thing was permitted to know that word. She turned to do the same to the party, and stayed her own hand; only when [[Ryo]] raised the ring on his finger did she understand why. *"I am [[Mirileth]],"* she told them, *"and also [[Ellesandra Silmara|Ellesandra]]"* — the very ancestor who, two thousand years past, had bargained her body away to topple the Hunt, and together with the archfae had trapped the archdevil [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]] inside the stone they now begged her for. She laid out her terms before the night closed: kill the crone and her coven, open for her the [[Vault of Memories]], and journey into the Hells to destroy the devil's reforming body once the soulstone is broken — and the anchor would be theirs.

## Details

### Into the Eternal Night

* The party took a long rest at the threshold, then debated their approach — agreeing the Vale was hostile-to-unfriendly territory and that they faced fae, not elves.
* **Character Moment:** [[Berberis]] reminded them of every prior fae encounter — brain-melting pixies, souls played for chess, memories traded for passage — and warned the price here would not be material.
* **Character Moment:** [[Ryo]] resolved to stay quiet and let the others talk, unwilling to reveal his royal Silmara lineage; he slapped [[Dondar]] for nearly blurting it to the Hunt later.
* They pushed through the black veil — cold, clinging, drawing the warmth from their bodies — and emerged into the silver-and-black landscape [[Kai]] had glimpsed the night before.
* [[Dondar]] summoned a bat familiar of his own; [[Kai]] summoned his piebald bat; [[Berberis]] called his owl.
* **Major Beat — the cursed river:** [[Dondar]]'s familiar dipped into the black [[River of Night]]; it surfaced greyed and shivering, its life-force stripped, and suffered a lingering curse (disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws).
* [[Kai]] tried to manipulate the river with magic and could not move it at all — it was not water, but something like fallen night draining away.
* Strong navigation rolls carried them swiftly down a valley alongside the river; they spotted a carved road on the far bank.
* They recharged their [[Zephyr Cuffs]] and glided across the river to reach the road, finding it churned with hoofprints and clawed paws — some twice the size of a hand.
* They passed glowing spore-mushrooms on the foothills and wisely left them alone.

### The Hunt

* Wolf-howls closed in from behind; the party chose to stand their ground rather than run.
* **The Hunt arrives:** a dire worg three or four times the size of a normal one rounded the bend, followed by a dozen armoured centaurs who encircled the party, spears levelled.
* Their leader was [[Caradoc]] — a tall, grey centaur with a long ponytail and an eyepatch over a ruined eye — flanked by his right-hand lieutenant.
* Caradoc demanded who they were and how they had come; [[Berberis]], boosted by [[Kai]]'s magic, claimed they were travellers from the Shining City sent to restore relations and pursue trade.
* Caradoc saw through the lie that they were not elf-blooded, noting [[Ryo]]'s features, but the party held to a tale of diplomacy and "something the world needs."
* **The Hunt rules the Vale:** Caradoc promised safe passage under his protection — *"nothing happens without our say-so"* — and took the party's weapons.
* **Character Moment:** [[Eryndor]] palmed his [[Renite Steel]] dagger with a flawless sleight of hand; [[Berberis]] kept his staff by asking, *"would you deny an old man his walking stick?"*
* The two centaurs argued heatedly in Sylvan as they marched; halfway down toward the crater, Caradoc overruled his lieutenant and turned the party off the road into the barrow-mounds.

### The Barrow-Crone's Bargain

* The party was led through webs thicker than fingers and past green eyes in the dark to a built-up stone mound, then down a cold tunnel into a cauldron-lit cavern.
* A hunched figure tending a bubbling green cauldron looked up; for an instant they glimpsed a hideous, claw-handed hag before the image resolved into a frail old woman.
* **Character Moment:** [[Kai]] whispered to [[Eryndor]] that he got "the same vibes" as the sea hag they had once faced.
* She conjured chairs of root that forced the party to sit and bound them in thorns that bit deeper whenever she was displeased (dealing piercing damage twice over the scene).
* **Major Beat — the slip:** when [[Berberis]] named the *soulstone anchor*, the crone's confident mask cracked with genuine shock before she recovered.
* She read [[Ryo]]'s heritage — pulling his hair, smelling it — and named him a returned Silmara, *"you who caused the night to fall in my lands."*
* She pressed her hands to the party's heads to sift their minds, and concluded they were not truly aligned with the "disgusting elves" of the Peaks.
* **The lie:** she painted the Queen as an evil tyrant preparing war upon the Shimmering Peaks, and herself as a wronged elder forced out to the [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City#The Barrow-Ring|The Barrow-Ring]].
* **The offer:** she produced a vial of dark liquid sealed into a root-and-wood amulet — worn into the audience and shattered at the Queen's feet, it would weaken her so the crone's faction could strike.
* **Major Plot Twist — insight:** a strong read told the party most of what she said was untrue, and that fae are magically bound to their bargains.
* She gave her name as [[Ymrageth]]; [[Eryndor]] negotiated assurances that they could leave the Vale alive and with the soulstone anchor.
* **The deal struck:** [[Berberis]] promised only to *see the amulet into the Queen's presence* — not to use it — and sealed it with the gift of a black rose.
* [[Caradoc]] was ordered to escort the party onward and ensure they kept their end of the bargain.

### Through the Dawnless City

* The centaurs led the party out of the barrows and back to the great Antler Gates, passing the party off as prisoners under escort.
* They moved through the Lower Warren — narrow streets full of skittering redcaps and goblin-things glaring from alleys — and up the rising avenue of the Midnight Procession.
* They looked down on the Muster Yards, vast kennels full of snarling shapes, and rose through the Court Tiers, where the people grew elven but unmistakably *other* — dark blue and purple skin, sharp angular features.
* **Character Moment:** [[Kai]] asked [[Caradoc|Caradoc]] for somewhere to wash up before meeting the Queen; the warden was baffled — *"you look like you've been on a hunt. It's good."*
* While they waited at the throne-room doors, [[Kai]] cleaned and healed the party with a sustained aura of restoring light.
* **Character Moment:** [[Berberis]] traded hunting stories with [[Caradoc|Caradoc]], who declared flatly that *"woods are for hunting"* and that he made no deals — *"I ride. I hunt. But I am honourable. I keep my word."*

### Audience with the Star-Veiled Queen

* The throne room was a long hall split by a fountain of bluish-black water, lit by moonlight through vast stained-glass windows; the lunar beam rose from the spire above.
* The Queen arrived as gusting mist, coalescing into a figure some ten feet tall — elven but wrong, with pitch-black eyes and a gown that shifted endlessly between white and black.
* She greeted them calmly, saying she had *"seen you coming,"* and asked why they had come.
* **Major Beat — betrayal repaid:** [[Berberis]] presented the crone's amulet and exposed [[Ymrageth]]'s plot; the Queen floated it over and dissolved it to dust.
* She noted with a smile how carefully [[Berberis]] had worded the bargain to avoid breaking it — *"she must be losing her edge."*
* When the party again spoke of the *soulstone anchor*, her composure broke into shock, then a cold and dangerous stillness.
* **Major Plot Twist — the killings:** the Queen crossed the hall and slew [[Caradoc]] and his lieutenant instantly — a hand driven into one skull, the other's head wrenched fully around — for hearing the forbidden word.
* She demanded who had told them; the party named the Silver Sage, [[Praetor'Varanous|Praetor]], and swore only they knew of it.
* She raised a lightning-wreathed hand to kill them all — and faltered, *"something stays my hand."*
* **Major Reveal — the ring:** [[Ryo]] showed (but would not remove) the ring on his hand; the Queen looked at it and softened — *"that explains why there was a part of me that didn't want to kill you."*

### The Queen's Secret

* **Major Reveal:** the Queen named herself [[Mirileth]] — *"and also [[Ellesandra Silmara|Ellesandra]]"* — two minds fused in one body, the source of the gown's shifting colours.
* She told her history: torn asunder long ago and left a wandering spirit, tormented by the archdevil [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]] of the Hells.
* Two thousand years past, a young [[Ellesandra Silmara|Silmara]] woman came to end the millennia-long war; [[Mirileth]] struck a bargain — she would topple the Hunt, and in return take the woman's body and the throne.
* Together they hunted [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]], destroyed his body, and sealed his soul inside the soulstone — the only prison from which an archdevil cannot simply reform in the Hells.
* **Major Lore — the soulstones:** ancient artifacts forged perhaps at creation's dawn to cage entities deemed mistakes, beings that could not be destroyed; few remain.
* The stone the party seeks is the one [[Mirileth]] guards most jealously — and it is already occupied by [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]].
* **The deal on the table:** kill [[Ymrageth]] and her coven; grant [[Mirileth]] entry to the [[Vault of Memories]] (which [[Ryo]]'s blood can open); and, once the stone is broken, travel to the Hells to destroy [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]]'s reforming body before he regains full strength (a matter of weeks or months).
* She offered to open a portal, or for [[Ryo]] to carry them via a World Gate — safer than planeshifting, which would further wound the already-weakened realms — but warned the way back was theirs to figure out.
* **Major Reveal — Asha:** pressed on returning the lost son of the Blade Lord to smooth relations with the Peaks, the Queen confirmed [[Asha Vayne]] yet lives in her court — *"you probably walked past him in the halls without knowing it."*
* **Character Moment:** [[Ryo]] grasped that the Star-Veiled Queen carries the blood of his own ancestor, his five-times-great grandmother.
* **Cliffhanger:** the deal laid bare — kill a crone, release and destroy a devil, and broker a royal pilgrimage between the courts of night and day — the party resolved to weigh it, with the World Gate waiting open ahead.
