## Summary

[[Ryo]] set the ring into the dais and the [[Vault of Memories]] opened. Partway down the long spiral stair, the girl Ellessa ran and leapt off the edge. [[Kai]] dove after her, and the two were caught in a swirl of glowing light that carried them gently down past drifting memories. The rest of the party followed, all but [[Dondar]], who had to climb back up a flight and jump before the vault would take him. The girl seemed puzzled that they had never come down this way: once the vault has accepted you, she said, it's the quickest road, and she had last been accepted about two thousand years ago. In the dragon's hall she ran straight to [[Praetor'Varanous|Praetor]] and gave him a potion from the magic shop. He drank it and began hiccupping puffs of snow. Then he looked down at her and said, *"Ellesandra."* *"You got old,"* she said. *"I was always old,"* he answered. *"You got small."*

The child grew tall, her short dark hair streaming out long and silver, until [[Ellesandra Silmara]] stood before them. She wept into the old dragon's arms after two thousand years apart. When she had dried her eyes she thanked the party and declared [[Mirileth]]'s bargain complete. She would release the devil whenever they were ready, they should go quickly when she did, and the stone would be theirs once they returned and sent word. She told them the Queen had never been with them on this journey, only Ellesandra herself, granted this brief time in exchange for the deal before returning to her vigil. Touching Ryo's face, she told him she saw a lot of him in her son and that the weight of expectation was not his to carry: *"It's still your free choice."* Then she faded on a wind that wasn't there. Praetor told them who she was: the old ruler of [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]], who walked alone into the darkness to end the war with the [[Vale of Eternal Night|Vale]] and is honoured as a hero who gave her life. In fact she has spent two thousand years holding that darkness at bay. Back in the city, a sending to [[Belinda Bubblegum|Belinda]] in [[Arkangel]] brought cheerful advice: guard your names, carry fire-resistance potions, bring food and water, and bring her a present.

Before midday Ryo opened the [[World Gates|World Gate]] beneath the [[Heartstone]], and the party stepped through into [[Avernus]]. They found a rust-red waste under a burning sky, littered with wrecked siege engines, and armies fighting on the horizon. The gate sealed behind them. Kai spotted a river at the foot of distant mountains, and they climbed down from the ridge and marched toward it. In a fuming gorge, a wounded, flame-winged warrior with a broken wing came limping toward them and called out, *"Mortals! Stand with me, and I will see you to the river! On my name and my wing, I swear it!"* Behind her came a hulking demon, swarms of snapping wretches, and screeching vulture-demons. Dondar's great conjured gauntlet shoved one of the vulture-demons into a lava pool. [[Eryndor]] drove a dagger up under the brute's jaw, and the erinyes cut the last vulture-demon apart once the party had it bound to the ground. Then she rounded on Kai, whose holy healing had burned her every time: *"Do not try that again here. If we had not made a bargain, I would slay you where you stand and take your souls with me."* She is keeping her word, though, and with the party's first pact in the Hells sealed, she set off at a jog for the river and told them to keep up.

## Details

### Down Through the Vault

* Ryo set the ring in the dais as before; the floor turned and a stone stair spiralled down into the cylindrical entry chamber.
* Eryndor watched over Ellessa in the middle of the group, with Kai at the very back.
* **Major Beat:** partway down, the girl bolted and leapt off the open edge. Eryndor's grab missed.
* Kai jumped after her, ready to activate his Steps of Night to fly once he caught her.
* Glowing spiral lines filled the chamber and slowed their fall to a gentle descent past hazy visions of memories, among them people drinking around a tavern table.
* Ellessa, confused: *"What, do you not always come down this way?"* Kai: the first time they had fought a golem.
* Kai used a sending to tell Eryndor to jump; Eryndor replied *"Are you crazy? You expect me to jump?"*, then stepped backwards off the ledge. Berberis followed with a backwards fall, arms spread.
* Dondar walked down the stairs, and the floor stayed shut for him at the bottom. He climbed a flight, jumped, and was caught and carried through too.
* The floor crumbled open beneath them, and they drifted past doorways and chambers from different eras, the girl seeming to tug them along.
* They landed in the vast columned hall where the dragon waits.
* Ellessa: once the vault has accepted you, this is the quickest way, and you can steer where you end up.
* Berberis asked when she had been accepted. *"Probably about 2000 years ago. That was the last time I was here."*
* Asked why she needed them to get in, she pointed at Ryo's ring: *"It's kind of complicated, but you'll probably soon see why."*
* Dondar: *"Please tell me you're not in love with the lizard."* She wrinkled her nose: *"Oh, no."* He's an old friend.

### The Gift and the Unveiling

* Praetor, in his robed old-man form with long silver hair and beard, stood at the glowing basin on the dais.
* Ellessa broke away and ran to him. Ryo cast longstrider and chased her, but his hands passed straight through her.
* She hugged Praetor around the waist and held up the potion she had taken from the magic shop: *"I brought you a present."*
* Praetor: *"Ah, my friends. I'm glad to see you return from the Vale."* He drank it in one long draught.
* **The Gift:** on Dondar's d4 roll of 1, Praetor got frost hiccups, each one a puff of snow.
* **Major Plot Twist:** Praetor: *"Ellesandra."* She: *"You got old."* He: *"I was always old. You got small."* She: *"Well, it was convenient for the time."*
* Her short black hair unfurled into long silver hair as she grew into a full-sized elven woman.
* Dondar: *"I had no idea. You fooled us all, your majesty."*
* Praetor: *"I felt you go. I waited for you to come back."* Ellesandra: *"I know. But I couldn't. I had to stay behind to keep the darkness in check."*
* *"Two thousand years, child." "I'm sorry I never came." "You came now."*
* She collapsed into his arms, sobbing. Praetor nodded to the party in thanks.
* She looked fragile afterwards, much like the woman they had seen in the Citadel in the Vale, a long-held tension loosening a little.

### Deal Complete

* **Major Beat:** *"Thank you. It might not seem like much, but you have no idea what you've given me here. As far as I am concerned, our bargain is complete."*
* *"Whenever you're ready, I will release him. Go quickly when I do, though. He will not stay helpless for long. And when you return, send us a message, and the stone is yours."*
* Kai asked who sits on the throne in the Vale while she's here. *"I'm not really here, is the thing. I am, but I'm not."* Her offered hand felt solid, then his passed through it.
* *"She has not been with us any of this time. It was just me."* It took convincing, but the Queen granted her this time as part of the deal.
* It's only temporary. She will go back and keep her vigil for as long as she must.
* **Character Moment:** to Ryo: *"I've seen you watching me. And I am sorry, for what it's worth."* Her touch on his face carried a rush of immense knowledge and experience.
* *"I see a lot of you in my son. Wish you would have met him… I know he would be proud of you."*
* *"You hold the weight of thousands of years. I hope you know that the weight of expectations don't go with that. It's still your free choice… just know that at least someone from your family is thinking of you."*
* She sat with Praetor on the edge of the dais trading old jokes, growing warmer, until she blew away piece by piece on a wind, mid-sentence.
* The party settled it: she releases the devil when they're ready, and the stone comes once they send word on their return.

### Praetor's Counsel

* Praetor apologised for any deception on his part. He hadn't known her fate, only hoped.
* The stone is still essential to bringing about the Crimson Shadow's demise, and that should stay their focus.
* **Lore:** Ellesandra Silmara, Ryo's ancestor and Sumara's former ruler, went alone into the darkness at the height of the war with the Vale and never returned. The war ended, and she is celebrated as a hero who sacrificed herself.
* *"Now we know… the terrors that she endures in order to hold the darkness at bay."* He told Ryo to be proud of his great-grandmother, a simplification for an ancestor many generations removed.
* Ryo asked whether she could be freed from Mirileth. *"All things are possible. But the question next would be, what happens if she is separated? What would Mirileth do?"*
* Ryo asked whether he has living relatives besides Eryndor's mother. *"None living, I'm afraid."*
* **Lore:** Kai asked whether devils fought in the [[Chaos Wars]]. They didn't: the conflict was confined to the material realm, and devils only profited from the souls and bargains. Kai said that changed his view of them.
* Ryo wants to reach the devil before he reforms. Riv confirmed that sending crosses planes.
* Riv recapped the Lectorium research: everyone arrives in Avernus, the battlefield of the Blood War; Dis, the Iron City, is the whole second layer; the devil is believed to be reforming there; and the lead is a theatre in the Rustward quarter.
* Avernus will be hot and draining over long travel, but not burning.
* Ryo asked whether unenchanted weapons would work. Praetor: many beings in the Hells resist them. Riv later ruled that 2024 fiends lack that resistance, so they're fine.

### A Message to Belinda

* Dondar didn't have sending prepared, so Kai sent it for him.
* The party last heard from [[Belinda Bubblegum|Belinda]] by a flying letter that smacked Dondar in the face on the Eastern Road: she had reached [[Arkangel]], the flying city of arcane scholars, using the rod of transportation she crafted in [[Lighthaven]].
* Dondar's message: *"Hey Belinda, how's life? Going to hell. Do you have any advice?"*
* Her reply: *"Great to hear from you. Never been to the hells myself. Don't give out your name for free. Stock up on potions of resistance of fire if you can. Make sure you bring some food and water. You might struggle to find some down there. Good luck. Let me know how it goes. Bring me a present."* Riv ruled that the long reply came as a couple of sendings.
* Kai suggested aliases: "General Slayer", with Ryo as the "Hag Slayer". Riv: everyone knows their own true name, and it matters only in certain places.
* Dondar wanted to borrow the [[Crone's Eye]], but it needs attunement.
* **Character Moment:** Kai let slip that Ryo and Eryndor have *"a weird aversion"* to Dondar. Dondar, stung, decided it could wait until they're back from Hell.

### Supplies

* Kai bought two shovels, two picks and two sledgehammers for the bag of holding.
* [[Arcanist Levvel]] had two potions of fire resistance at 300 gp each; Kai and Eryndor bought them.
* Ryo bought the city's one greater healing potion for 200 gp, plus a waterskin and a dozen rations. Kai's decanter, create water and goodberry cover the rest.
* Fire-resistant clothing exists but is rare and expensive, and is kept for the war effort.
* Ryo threatened to cast silence on Dondar every time he calls him royalty.
* Berberis sought holy water for protection from evil and good. Sumara's elves are secular and their god is the [[World Tree]], so there's little to be had. He switched to another spell.
* Kai planned to change coin into silver at the elven exchange to make holy water: 25 gp of powdered silver and an hour per vial.

### The Grimoire's First Spell

* Dondar spent an hour with [[Ymrageth's Grimoire]], its letters dancing away from wherever he looked, leaving him dizzy and headachy.
* **Reward Obtained:** he passed the intelligence save, and the book revealed a fourth-level spell that he can copy into his own spellbook.
* The effort left him nauseous at the thought of trying again that day. The fingers around the book's edges clasped shut as he put it away.

### Before the Gate

* Kai asked whether they should tell anyone they were leaving.
* Dondar suggested Ryo secure the royal bloodline before going; Ryo declined.
* Ryo joked about opening the gate to [[Glimmerforge]].
* Riv: some in Sumara would want to stop the only person who can open the World Gates from going to the Hells, but [[Illythia Voss|Illythia]] would point out that without Ryo the rest of them couldn't get home.
* The Council still has to discuss Ryo's strike force for [[Val Miriel]] and unsealing an operative in the dwarven kingdom for Dondar.
* The Heartstone is guarded at its bridge, but the World Gate chamber itself was dusty and empty. Kai cleaned it in patches with prestidigitation.

### Into Avernus

* The gate tore open on shimmering heat and a dark red wasteland, and the party stepped through into [[Avernus]], the first layer of the [[Nine Hells]].
* The air went from mild to very hot: uncomfortable, but not dangerous.
* **First Sight:** a blazing sky, crushed siege engines across the waste, beasts clashing overhead and whole armies fighting on the horizon.
* On a 26 perception check, Kai felt cut off from his god as the gate zipped shut behind them. From the ridge he spotted mountains far off, with the cut of a river at their base.
* Riv on the gates: gate-to-gate travel always connects, and anywhere else is a roll like plane shift. The Hells are different: everyone arrives in Avernus, which is why Ryo didn't roll.
* Dondar wondered whether leaving the ring in the dais would hold a gate open. The others thought a permanent portal to Hell was a bad idea.
* Kai kept the party cool with a light rain from his decanter and shape water.
* They descended the ridge, a couple of hundred feet, by rope of climbing. Riv ruled that the rope must fasten to something: *"The rope of climbing is not a broomstick."*
* Riv confirmed that flying broomsticks do exist in the world. Dondar wants one.
* They marched past house-sized trebuchets still smouldering with embers, and pools of bubbling lava, into a gorge thick with fumes.

### The Erinyes

* Heavy footsteps approached. Ryo cast pass without trace, and the party took cover behind rocks.
* Out of the fog came a tall armoured figure with a full plate helm, blazing horns, wings of fire and a glowing coiled rope at her waist. She was limping, and one wing hung broken.
* **Major Beat:** *"Mortals! Stand with me, and I will see you to the river! On my name and my wing, I swear it!"*
* Berberis: local guides are never a bad idea. Ryo: *"We fight."* Dondar asked her name, but there was no time for an answer.
* Kai shouted to ask whether her pursuers were friend or foe. *"Foe! Demon filth! Slay them!"*

### Battle in the Gorge

* The foes: a hezrou, three swarms of dretches, and vrocks that swooped in out of the smoke.
* Ryo used Alert to swap initiative with Kai, who opened with Moonspear Descent, his moonbeam variant, on two dretch swarms, then took his chalice starry form.
* A hidden vrock's stunning screech stunned Berberis and Dondar. Eryndor resisted.
* Eryndor hit the vrock with the [[Emberblade]] for 20, spending a charge for radiant damage, then landed a sneak attack with his dagger.
* The hezrou caught the erinyes and raked her back. Its stench poisoned Kai, but she shrugged it off.
* Ryo put two arrows into the vrock with Hunter's Mark, leaving it bloodied.
* **Character Moment:** Kai's Healing Word on the erinyes mended her wing, but its lunar light hissed and burned her skin. *"Holy. Keep that to yourself."*
* Eryndor used Shield for the first time and turned both of the vrock's attacks aside.
* Berberis cast a fourth-level Thunderwave on two dretch swarms and the hezrou. All three failed, taking 21 each, 63 in total, and were hurled back into the jagged cliffs.
* Berberis charged his new staff with Shillelagh: it now glows green with leaves, and topple and bloom no longer cost charges.
* A second vrock's screech stunned Kai, breaking his concentration, and hit the erinyes and Ryo.
* **Major Beat:** Dondar's Bigby's Hand, a huge purple gauntlet in dwarven armour, shoved the bloodied vrock 25 feet down into a lava pool. It took 18d10 fire and sank screeching. Riv called it possibly the biggest single damage roll of the campaign.
* The hezrou leapt behind the line and landed a critical hit that dropped the erinyes to one knee.
* Berberis struck the hezrou with Zephyr Strike, Shillelagh and Hunter's Prey, leaving it bloodied.
* A vrock seized Dondar and carried him 10 feet up. He misty stepped onto the ridge, then hit it with a fourth-level fireball, which it partly resisted.
* Ryo hit the vrock with arrows and Hail of Thorns.
* Kai's Aura of Vitality burned the erinyes again on a failed check. *"I'm so sorry. I'm trying to help."*
* **Major Beat:** Eryndor burned three Emberblade charges, pinned the hezrou's arm with his rapier, and drove his dagger up under its jaw for 24 sneak-attack damage, killing it.
* The erinyes nodded to him: *"Good. That one is dead. Now just these pests."*
* Her glowing rope lashed around a vrock's wings and it plummeted. *"Focus on killing one of them at a time. Spread out so that you aren't affected by their disgusting poisons or screeches."*
* Berberis's Land's Aid raised gnarled native brambles that sapped the vrock and healed the erinyes. Nature magic didn't burn her. A maximum Healing Word followed.
* Another screech stunned Dondar, Eryndor and Kai.
* Ryo critically hit the roped vrock with Hunter's Mark and Hail of Thorns, and the erinyes split its head with a two-handed blow.
* Berberis's Ensnaring Strike and topple left the last vrock restrained and prone. Riv offered to end the fight, and the party chose to finish it.
* Kai rang Toll of the Dead and Eryndor added a sneak attack. The erinyes did roughly 90 damage, taking off one wing, then the other, then the head.

### The Pact

* She strode straight up to Kai: *"Do not try that again here. If we had not made a bargain, I would slay you where you stand and take your souls with me."*
* *"But I must relent. A pact was made. And I will stand by it. Let's make this quick. I have soldiers on the battlefield waiting for me."*
* The demons carried no weapons or gear. Berberis yanked some pin feathers from a vrock as they left.
* **Reward Obtained:** Ryo hacked off the hezrou's massive hand, which takes both arms to carry, and handed it to Kai for the bag of holding.
* Dondar's Bigby's Hand followed them for its last minute.
* **Cliffhanger:** *"Come. I assume you seek the river. Try to keep up."* The erinyes jogged off toward the gorge without looking back, leading the party toward the Styx.
