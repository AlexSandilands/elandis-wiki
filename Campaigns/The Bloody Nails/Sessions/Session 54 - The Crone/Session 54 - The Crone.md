## Summary

Hidden among a cluster of rocks in the barrow-ring, wrapped in [[Ryo]]'s trace-hiding magic, the party made their final preparations for the assault on the crone's den. Rather than walk in through the mouth of the barrow, they climbed its mound and found what they had hoped for — a small opening at its crown, a narrow tunnel breathing noxious fumes up out of the dark. The trusty rope of climbing carried them down while [[Berberis]] slipped ahead in the shape of a spider, clinging to the ceiling of the cavern below. There [[Kai]] and Berberis spied something unexpected on the den floor: three soldiers of the Citadel bound and helpless — and one of them was [[Evrath]], the pale-skinned knight who had guarded their door.

Kai flew down to cut the captives loose, and as he worked the voice of [[Ymrageth]] drifted through the den, honeyed and reasonable. They were making a mistake, it said — the Queen was the evil one, scheming to reignite the old war; she wanted into the [[Vault of Memories]] so she could tear down the protections of the [[Liar's River]] and destroy [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]] from within. Side with her instead, the voice promised, and she would deliver them the soulstone — no strings attached. When the party gave her lies no answer, she called them fools. Then the trap closed: the soldier Kai had freed took the knife he offered — and twisted back into a night hag, driving the blade into his chest. All three "captives" vanished and reappeared on the raised platforms deep in the cavern, and the barrow erupted. Waking nightmares clawed at Kai and Ryo, black tentacles burst from the ground to hold the party fast, and the crone herself flickered from shadow to shadow, impossible to pin — though Berberis hounded her relentlessly, staff cracking against her again and again, while [[Dondar]]'s fire blossomed through the coven, [[Eryndor]]'s [[Emberblade]] carved through hag-flesh, and Kai swept the cavern ringed in guardian spirits.

When the last of her three coven-sisters fell, Ymrageth played her final trick: she raised aloft a doll worked in Ryo's own likeness, woven from the hair stolen from him only the day before. But his will proved firm, her magic broke against it, and as her frustration curdled Ryo loosed a barrage of arrows that took the crone's head from her shoulders. In the quiet after, Eryndor picked through the den and found its treasures strewn among horrors — the skeletons of children, mounted skulls, flayed beasts — a hoard where every prize had been someone's payment. Stranger still was the Emberblade itself: it had burned longer than it should have with each thunderous strike of the battle, and even now, the fighting done, it went on humming and glowing with magic that would not settle. The session closed with the party bent over the crone's belongings, teasing out the power held in each one.

## Details

### Into the Barrow

* The session opened with the party hidden among a cluster of rocks in the barrow-ring, concealed by pass without a trace, making last-minute preparations for the assault.
* Rather than use the den's mouth, they investigated the top of the barrow mound for another way in.
* They found a small opening at the crown of the mound — a narrow tunnel, with noxious fumes drifting up from below.
* The party descended by rope of climbing, while [[Berberis]] wild-shaped into a spider and crept onto the inside roof of the den.

### The Crone's Lies

* [[Kai]] and Berberis spotted three Royal Citadel soldiers tied up on the cavern floor — one of them [[Evrath]], the pale-skinned soldier from the Citadel.
* Kai flew down and began cutting their bonds while the disembodied voice of [[Ymrageth]] tried to reason with the party.
* **Her claims:** the Queen is the truly evil one — she seeks to restart the war, and wants inside the [[Vault of Memories]] so she can drop the protections of the [[Liar's River]].
* **The offer:** if the party sided with her instead, she would get them the soulstone — no strings attached.
* When the party refused to engage with her, she called them fools.
* **Major Plot Twist:** Kai freed a soldier and handed him a knife — and the "soldier" abruptly transformed back into a night hag and stabbed him in the chest.
* All three captives vanished and reappeared on the raised platforms deeper in the cavern, initiating the fight.

### Hags of the Barrow

* [[Kai]] and [[Ryo]] were afflicted with waking nightmares (Phantasmal Killer).
* Black tentacles erupted from the ground, grasping and holding several of the party in place.
* [[Ymrageth]] was hard to lock down, using her deathly teleport legendary action to flicker across the den.
* **Character Moment:** [[Berberis]] stayed on top of the crone throughout, hammering her with shillelagh and Topple weapon mastery to drag her down.
* [[Dondar]] hurled fireballs into the night hags, [[Eryndor]] slashed through them with the [[Emberblade]], and Kai flew the cavern wreathed in spirit guardians.
* **Mystery — the Emberblade:** the blade's glow lingered far longer than normal after each booming blade was cast, and even after the fight it continued to hum and glow with magical energy.
* All three night hags of the coven were slain.

### The Effigy

* With her coven dead, [[Ymrageth]] revealed her final trick — a doll made in [[Ryo]]'s form, woven from his real hair taken the previous day, raised high above him.
* **Major Beat — the will of the Silmara:** Ryo's wisdom held firm against her magic, and the effigy failed, to the crone's mounting frustration.
* **Character Moment:** Ryo ended the fight himself, taking Ymrageth's head off with a barrage of arrows.

### The Barrow-Hoard

* [[Eryndor]] investigated the den, finding treasures strewn among horrific trophies — skeletons of children, mounted skulls, and flayed animals.
* **Reward Obtained:** [[Ymrageth's Grimoire]] — the crone's spellbook, locked behind her dream-script.
* **Reward Obtained:** a crooked black-thorn staff of the woodlands — a trophy taken from a wood-power the crone unmade — claimed by [[Berberis]].
* **Reward Obtained:** the soul-debt hoard — 3,000 gp in gems worked into the den's walls, each stone payment on one of the crone's bargains.
* **Reward Obtained:** a scroll case of three scrolls — Seeming, Summon Fey, and Summon Fiend — taken by [[Dondar]].
* **Reward Obtained:** the Crone's Eye, a crystal ball of smoke-grey crystal — the instrument behind Ymrageth's long watching.
* **Reward Obtained:** the sword and quiver looted from the shop in [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]] were also identified — a +1 shortsword, and a quiver holding six +1 arrows and four +2 arrows.
* The session ended with the group identifying the magical items found in the treasure hoard; the grimoire, gems, and crystal ball remain unallocated.
