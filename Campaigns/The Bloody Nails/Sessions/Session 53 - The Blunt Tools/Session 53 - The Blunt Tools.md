## Summary

In the throne room of the Veiled Citadel, with the bodies of the slain centaurs still cooling beside the black channel, the party weighed the Star-Veiled Queen's bargain. [[Kai]] sent word across the leagues to [[Praetor'Varanous|Praetor]], who answered that the Queen's request to enter the [[Vault of Memories]] was a piece of a puzzle fitting more perfectly than he expected — inadvisable, perhaps, but ultimately the [[Silmara Family|Silmara]]'s decision, and so the choice fell to [[Ryo]]. [[Mirileth]] swept the dead into the river of night with a flick of her fingers and refused Kai's plea to spare the crone: she had known of [[Ymrageth]]'s scheming for an age, but the politics of her court were too delicate for her own hand to be seen in it — that was precisely why she needed outsiders. She armed them instead with knowledge: the crone keeps three lesser hags always at her side, mightier together, vulnerable apart.

The Queen granted them chambers high in the Citadel, and the night that followed was equal parts wonder and farce. Redcap servants laid out a feast of strange fae fare, [[Dondar]] drank himself insensible on midnight mead and was left to sleep on the balcony, and the party slowly befriended their door-guard — a quiet knight named [[Evrath]], whose skin showed pale where every other soldier's ran storm-grey, and who shivered, almost imperceptibly, when [[Ryo]] asked whether he had ever heard the name [[Asha Vayne|Asha]] — a name he then denied knowing. In the deep of night Ryo dreamed of a silver-haired figure in a white gown whose face he could not fix, and a voice that pleaded: *do not trust the Queen*. By morning the Queen's promised gift had settled over them all — to every eye, five soldiers of the Citadel in gleaming black obsidian armour.

The disguise carried them into the [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City#The Court Tiers|Court Tiers]], and there the day came apart. In an arcane shop whose keeper was forging enchanted swords for the city's armies, the party laid material-realm gold on the counter — coin no soldier of the Vale would carry — and the old shopkeeper slipped out the back and returned with a squad of the Citadel's black-armoured soldiers. Caught mid-looting and marched in manacles back before the throne, they watched [[Mirileth]] scrub the memory of the arrest from her own soldiers' minds with a thrown rune, then deliver a warning colder than the room: *do not disappoint me a second time — there will not be a second chance.* Chastened, the party marched out through the Midnight Procession in good soldier's order, followed a red-capped phantom off the road where their disguises fell away, and crept unseen through the barrow-mounds to the mouth of the crone's den — where they stand now, blades loosened, a hundred feet from the dark.

## Details

### The Queen's Terms

* The session opened in the throne room, [[Mirileth]] seated legs crossed on the dais, awaiting the party's answer with [[Caradoc]] and his lieutenant dead on the floor.
* [[Kai]] used sending magic to reach [[Praetor'Varanous|Praetor]]: *"Hello Praetor, we're in a pickle, the queen here has the stone, she wants to meet, everything wants to kill us."*
* Praetor replied he was glad they had not come to violence, and asked where the Queen wished to meet.
* Kai's second message revealed she wanted access to the vault — *"she's also Ryo's grandma."*
* **Major Beat — the Silmara's decision:** Praetor answered that this was *"a piece of the puzzle that fits more perfectly than I expected"* — letting her into the vault was inadvisable, but ultimately the [[Silmara Family|Silmara]]'s decision, leaving the choice with [[Ryo]].
* The Queen flicked her fingers and the two centaur bodies slid into the black channel, washing away to nothing.
* **Character Moment:** [[Kai]] proposed sparing [[Ymrageth]] — transforming her into something small and cute, contract-bound to protect Ryo — and the Queen admitted she liked the idea.
* **The refusal:** she has known of the crone's plotting for ages but the court's politics are in constant flux — *"I need the crones. I cannot be seen actively being malicious towards them"* — which is exactly why outsiders must do the deed and take the blame.
* **The intel:** Ymrageth keeps three lesser hags with her at all times; within throwing distance their power joins, and separating them makes them vulnerable.
* She offered an illusion to make the party appear as high-ranking members of her court, lasting most of a day, plus chambers in the Citadel for the night.
* **Character Moment:** when [[Eryndor]] muttered *"they always say they'll call back, but they don't,"* a voice sounded directly over his shoulder — *"I always will"* — though the Queen never appeared to speak.
* [[Dondar]] asked the Queen to help his river-cursed bat; she beckoned it to her hand and stroked the grey from its fur, noting a simple decurse spell handles the [[River of Night]]'s touch.
* A knight in black obsidian armour was summoned to escort them; a sharp eye caught that his skin was pale, not the dark grey of the city's elves.
* [[Ryo]] conjured the scents of Sumara's streets around the empty throne as they left — and a black rose formed silently in his hand.

### A Night in the Citadel

* The party was housed in a guest apartment high in the Citadel, with a wide window over the moonlit black spires of the city.
* Their guard gave his name only reluctantly: *"You can call me... [[Evrath]]."*
* Redcap servants in fine black linen delivered a feast of unfamiliar fae food — glowing mushrooms, green-tinged bread, purple root vegetables, and dark, potent mead.
* [[Kai]] ritual-cast a ward against poison and disease and swept the room — everything was clean.
* **Character Moment:** [[Eryndor]] and [[Ryo]] befriended Evrath, who sheepishly admitted a fondness for the purple root vegetables and accepted a plate — clearly unused to guests treating a guard kindly.
* [[Ryo]] quietly cast an alarm on the chamber door the moment they were alone.
* **Major Beat — the flinch:** when Ryo asked Evrath if he had ever heard the name [[Asha Vayne|Asha]], a keen eye caught a shiver run through the guard's body before he denied ever hearing it.
* **Character Moment:** [[Dondar]] chugged the fae mead, got roaring drunk, vomited, and passed out; Eryndor left him on the balcony with a pillow and blanket.
* The party checked their sending stones — no new messages; ships from Val Noren are already sailing to reinforce Val Miriel.
* **Major Beat — the dream:** during the night [[Ryo]] dreamed of a figure with long silvery hair, elven features, and a white gown, its face impossible to grasp, that pleaded *"Ryo, do not trust the queen..."* before washing away; he woke remembering every detail, and kept it to himself.

### Soldiers of the Citadel

* Morning brought joffy all round — including an extra-strong mug for Evrath, who had stood guard all night.
* **Character Moment:** [[Dondar]] woke hungover from a nightmare and reflexively hurled a firebolt off the balcony, drawing shouts from the streets below.
* Stepping out, the party discovered the Queen's gift had taken hold: all five appeared as dark-elven Citadel soldiers in black obsidian armour — pure illusion, as a hand passed straight through it.
* [[Evrath]] recoiled in shock, then decided *"I'm not going to question it,"* and taught them the Citadel salute — fist to chest, halberd butt to the ground.
* On the walk down, other soldiers glared at Evrath as he passed, eyes following him in silence.
* Evrath left them at the great landing doors, his duty — as far as he was concerned — complete.

### The Shopping Trip

* The party found a marketplace on the eastern Court Tiers, where well-dressed nobles parted and stared at the marching "soldiers" — their poor imitation of military bearing drawing suspicious eyes.
* Inside an arcane shop, the old grey-skinned shopkeeper assumed they had come for weapons: *"I told your captain yesterday... I'm working as fast as I can"* — the city is having enchanted swords forged to bolster its arms.
* [[Eryndor]] asked him to identify the Raven Dagger of Warning and the spider amulet from [[Nelfandriss the Banished|Nelfandriss]], laying gold coins on the counter as payment.
* **Major Beat — the tell:** the coins stopped the shopkeeper cold; no Citadel soldier would carry material-realm currency, and he excused himself "for silver powder" and never returned.
* The party responded by looting the shop — a sword and quiver of arrows from the back workshop, a crate of potions, and a pair of boots.
* Half a dozen soldiers burst in, halberds levelled, and named them either traitors or outsiders; Eryndor's silver tongue failed him for once.
* The party submitted to arrest; the manacles passed clean through Dondar's illusory arms, betraying the magic.
* They were marched through gawking noble crowds — the shopkeeper watching from across the market — and back up to the Citadel.

### Before the Throne Again

* The captain knelt and reported the outsiders' theft to [[Mirileth]], who kept a steely neutral face — betraying only the slightest sigh.
* **Major Beat — the memory wipe:** the Queen traced a rune in the air, crushed it, and threw it; purple light flashed through the soldiers' eyes, and she dismissed them as though nothing had happened.
* **Character Moment:** [[Berberis]] owned the disaster — *"when it comes to war and killing, we're quite good... we are blunt tools"* — and the Queen conceded that *"sometimes a blunt tool is what is required."*
* **The warning:** *"Do not disappoint me a second time. There will not be a second chance."* She evaporated, and the disguises settled back over them.
* The stolen potions and boots were surrendered; the sword and quiver stayed quietly in the bag of holding.

### Return to the Barrow-Ring

* Drilled into a proper marching column by [[Berberis]], the party crossed the city convincingly this time, passing riders on great black steeds with manes of smoke and hoofprints of embers.
* They passed the gates without challenge and headed up the main road out of [[Caer Nystral, The Dawnless City|Caer Nystral]].
* **Major Beat — the phantom redcap:** a freshly-red-capped figure called them off the road — *"hey captain, I've got that report"* — led them behind the boulders, and vanished; their disguises dropped, apparently the Queen's way of ending the masquerade unseen.
* [[Kai]]'s bat swept the area with blindsight and [[Dondar]] ritual-cast a detection of magic — nothing but the ambient wisps of the Faewild.
* Kai tested his shield's moon-sense: it tugged in every direction at once, spinning uselessly.
* With [[Ryo]]'s pass-without-trace magic the party ghosted through the barrow-mounds, retracing [[Caradoc]]'s route from the previous day.
* Kai laid powerful restorative wards, bolstering himself, Berberis, and [[Eryndor]] for the fight ahead.
* They noted the cracks and root-choked openings in the den's cavern roof — a possible way in from above.
* **Cliffhanger:** the party halted a hundred feet from the mouth of [[Ymrageth]]'s den, weapons ready, the assault on the crone and her coven waiting for the next session.
