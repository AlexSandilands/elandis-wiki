## Summary

With the soul storm boiling over the horizon, the party let [[Seraxis]] wrap rusted chains about their necks and ran at her heels into the camp of the Third Talon, a fortress of iron and spikes where faceless soldiers drilled as one and lemures by the hundred hauled a screaming war machine. Her commander, Ashvere, dropped from the central spire to meet them, berated Seraxis for losing her whole patrol, and warned that she would be audited for her short soul quota. Seraxis claimed the five mortals as her own, souls bargained for with no term named, and offered them to be sent down the river and banked in the Talon's name. Ashvere turned to [[Berberis]] and demanded, *"Fodder or the river?"*, and he chose the river. Stripped of their gear, the party rode out the storm in hide-wrapped cages hanging over the Styx, beside a whimpering devil awaiting its unmaking and an unconscious old man in the red robes of a [[Voice of the Empire]]. Through it all [[Eryndor]] quietly reeled in a warm, palm-sized coin that had fallen from the storm, its embossed face never quite holding still.

When a ferry lit by a green lamp came down the river, Seraxis kept her word. At the dock she struck off their chains, returned their belongings, and lifted her helm to reveal a face of unsettling beauty with burning eyes. She warned them that the balance of power in the city is fragile, and that others would have abused the trust they had placed in her. She would not join them, but bid them luck: *"Maybe you could join me on the battlefield one of these days."* Berberis gave her a thorned rose and whispered the name of the Destroyer, and she admitted it was not the first time she had heard it, nor the first talk of larger wars to come. [[Ryo]] knelt and pressed gold into her hand as a gift. *"You are certainly a lot of fools, aren't you? But I do appreciate your kindness and your respect."* A fussy imp in a little jacket then hurried them aboard, and each paid the fare in gold under a false name.

On deck waited [[Sahrim]], a tiger-headed agent of the House of Provenance, who pried at their secrets with easy charm. He saw through Ryo's denials, plucked the name of [[Nelfandriss the Banished]] from Eryndor's thoughts, and coveted the [[Amulet of the Arachnid Queen]], warning that a dormant power inside it waits for some trigger to wake. When Berberis showed him the copied dragon of the [[Red Priests]] and told him freely that *"he's waking up and the worlds are shattering,"* Sahrim offered to introduce them to his master. Below deck, Berberis coaxed the red-robed mage back to consciousness. He was Lucan Merrow, who asked no questions of them and wanted none in return. A fallen knight in black armour recognised the moon on [[Kai]]'s shield: *"I used to wear one."* He recoiled from it and told Kai to cover it, and Kai inked the symbol out. At last the river ran through the mountains to the Chain Gate, a wall of iron a thousand feet high. There an erinyes captain read the manifest aloud and demanded holy articles be declared. Kai signed an oath, under his false name, never to use his shield or holy water while in the city, on pain of his soul. Then the chains rose, and the ferry slid on toward Dis.

## Details

### The Chains

* Kai spotted the fortress walls about half an hour away as the soul storm began to whip sand and grit around them.
* The storm was a rolling wall of red-black cloud split by red lightning.
* Berberis wanted assurances. Seraxis: *"No terms have changed and the agreement still stands. I agree to take you safely to the riverbank."* They could end the agreement and find their own way.
* Kai rolled two 28s on Arcana: the chains were unmagical rusted iron that Seraxis had torn from a siege engine during the battle.
* Berberis: *"Do you have any plans to betray us, sell us, enslave us, turn us into your thralls or proxies?"*
* Seraxis: *"My current plans are to get you safely to the river and aligned with passage down the river on a boat."* Once aboard, *"I will not help you and I will not impede you."*
* The party wrapped and latched the heavy chains around their own necks, and Seraxis tightened them with a yank.
* She is about seven feet tall. She ran them across the plain in tow, the storm's gusts lashing them with searing heat.

### The Camp of the Third Talon

* Barbed devils on the black spiked palisade opened the gate just wide enough for single file.
* Inside: rank on rank of faceless soldiers in blank iron masks, drilling as one.
* Lemures by the hundred hauled a smoking war machine. Each time devils fed something into its furnace, it screamed.
* Wounded devils lay in rows by the river, being stitched rather than healed. Imps watched from every spike and gutter.
* Barbed devils at the gate eyed the party, saw Seraxis, and pointedly looked away. *After all, you are property.*

### Commander Ashvere

* An erinyes with a black iron command crest dropped from the central spire, cracking the iron of the parade ground, and stood taller even than Seraxis.
* Ashvere: *"Seraxis, you are late, and your patrol is not with you."* Seraxis took a knee.
* Seraxis: *"We were ambushed. The whole party was lost. I barely survived. But I did. I was not deserting."*
* Ashvere accused her of wanting to start her own company, then ruled: *"You shall be audited. Your soul quota is coming in short."*
* Seraxis yanked their chains: *"In return for my protection, they put on chains of their own. Foolish. However, unfortunately for them, they named no length of term. They're mine for as long as I please."*
* Ashvere: *"Field plunder belongs to the company."* Seraxis: *"This is not plunder. It is a bargain made by me, for me."*
* **The Offer:** Seraxis offered them as a tithe, sent down the river and banked under the Talon's name, or as fodder for the line.
* Ashvere pointed to Berberis: *"You, dirty one! What say you? Fodder or the river?"* Berberis: *"I've done enough fighting today,"* with a flicker of a smile.
* Ashvere: *"So you wish to take the river. Give your soul to be spent cowardly."*
* She eyed their gear for the war effort. Seraxis: *"They are entirely mine, Commander. The bargain was made. That includes their belongings."*
* Ashvere claimed she was *"merely testing"*, told Seraxis to come and speak to her when it was done, and flew back to her spire. Seraxis let out the barest hint of relief.

### The Coin in the Storm

* As the storm broke over the walls, every crack of lightning carried screams, and clods of rock and dirt rained down.
* The lemures were lowest of devils, purchased souls set to work for generations. Devils can be demoted to lemures for bad behaviour or a botched bargain.
* With a passive Perception of 20, Ryo and Eryndor saw something shiny fall from the sky just off their path.
* Eryndor sent an invisible telekinetic Mage Hand, with no verbal or somatic components, to drag it along the ground like blown sand.
* An imp rolled a natural 20 and watched the hand intently. It then followed the party from rooftop to rooftop.
* Eryndor passed a Deception check with advantage from the storm.
* Kai watched for anyone taking an interest in Seraxis, and spotted other erinyes leaning in barracks doorways, watching her pass.

### Property Has No Need for Property

* They passed Ashvere's spire, the infirmary, and holding pens full of prodded demons, a few humans, tieflings and devils.
* Seraxis: *"Property has no need for property. Leave your belongings here."* Lemures brought crates.
* Kai leaned in to whisper to her and was backhanded for 2 damage: *"Property does not speak!"*
* *"You mortals. Your arrogance… Well, I guess we've only got eternity to teach you, don't we?"*
* Weapons, armour and bags went into the crates and were carted off. Kai kept his necklace, Ryo his ring, Berberis his cloak, and Eryndor the thieves' tools sewn into his left shirt cuff.

### The Cages

* Lemures wrapped two hanging iron cages in thick hide against the storm, 50 or 60 feet above the black, churning Styx.
* Kai and Ryo shared one cage; Berberis, [[Dondar]] and Eryndor the other.
* In an unprotected cage, a spined devil chained by all four limbs whimpered over and over: *"Unmade, remade."*
* Another hide-wrapped cage held a figure they couldn't see.
* Eryndor's Investigation of 25 found a moment when no one was watching, and he brought the object inside.
* **Reward Obtained:** a coin about four inches across, warm to the touch, embossed with a face that seemed to shift as he traced it. Eryndor pocketed it, hidden.
* Kai cast Message through his fox-fang druidic focus to confirm he could still cast without his gear.
* Riv confirmed Kai's druidic and divine magic flow from the same source, [[Selûne]], who chose him. Kai's starry form is unique to her.
* **Berberis's Instinct:** on a strong Insight check he felt Seraxis had technically kept every word, but likely held some positive regard for them. She had told Ashvere she was sending them down the river.
* Berberis, aloud: *"Patience."* Then he put his feet up and napped.
* The party took a short rest; no long rest was possible in the cages.

### The Red Robe

* Ryo and Kai (with Guidance) spotted a green glow far down the river, slowly drawing closer.
* A gust lifted the hides, and Eryndor glimpsed red cloth in the next cage. He warned Berberis it might be a Hand of the Empire.
* Berberis summoned his familiar as a fiendish weasel and tossed it across the four-foot gap onto the other cage.
* Inside was an unconscious elderly man with a long grey beard, stripped of belongings, in a red robe like the one [[Marcelle the Broken]] had worn.
* **Discovery:** not a Hand but a [[Voice of the Empire]], one of the Empire's mages. His robe bore an emblem of a sun behind a sextant that Berberis didn't recognise.
* The man stirred but did not wake. The weasel stayed to watch him.

### Seraxis's Farewell

* The ferry arrived, a green lantern humming at its prow, and Seraxis came down from the barracks to the gangplank.
* Barbed devils hauled the cages in, prodded the party out with spears, and Seraxis chained them again.
* Berberis slipped his weasel into a pocket of the mage's robe as barbed devils carried him aboard.
* Ryo understood the ferry imp's Infernal: the mage's passage and place on the manifest had been arranged and paid in advance.
* Seraxis dropped their chains: *"As per my word, the river, the ferry, and here you are safely."*
* She asked them to keep the details of their arrangement to themselves, for both their sakes. *"Some things are frail around here."*
* Ryo asked if gold had value here. *"Many of us long for it. Not I, of course. I only long for the battlefield."*
* She shielded them from view while they recovered their gear from the crates.
* **Major Beat:** she lifted off her helm, revealing a strikingly beautiful face, burning eyes, slightly pointed teeth, and black hair smouldering to embers at the tips.
* *"Don't make agreements and bargains unless you have to. The balances of power in the city are fragile. If one falls, another will take its place. If one is propped up, another will likely fall."*
* *"It was foolish for you to agree to be chained and led through the camp. There are others who would have abused that trust that you placed in me."*
* *"Remember, do not make such agreements again lightly. Not everyone would be as nice as me when you meet them, despite their appearances."*
* Kai asked her to join them. His Persuasion of 19 fell well short of a DC around 35.
* Seraxis: *"Even if I wanted to, it would be impossible."* She has a duty and cannot leave now of all times.
* Her deal with the ferryman is binding; as far as Ashvere knows, she is making one now.
* *"Maybe you could join me on the battlefield one of these days. Surely it's more interesting here than wherever else you call home."* Ryo: *"Perhaps after they're done."* *"Well, you know where to find me."*
* **Character Moment:** Berberis drew a large, thorny rose from his staff for her, then whispered: *"Worlds are colliding and things are changing. There is a much bigger battlefield than the one here."*
* He whispered her the name of the Destroyer, freely given.
* **Major Beat:** *"It's not the first time I've heard that name. And it's not the first time I've heard about larger wars on the way. Well, maybe things are about to get exciting."*
* Ryo knelt and offered her 20 gold as a gift, no strings. She checked he meant no malice, such as scrying, then took it on his Persuasion.
* *"You are certainly a lot of fools, aren't you? But I do appreciate your kindness and your respect. I know that you have mine as well, as odd as that sounds."*
* She put her helm back on, flames rekindling, and left them on the dock.

### The Fare

* An imp in a little suede jacket, with a bark board and charcoal: *"Come on, come on… I don't see any of you on the manifest."*
* **Lore:** passage to Dis costs 500 gold, a soul coin, a memory, a story, or a true name. Every passenger must have a name on the register.
* A story must be true, and chosen by the ferryman. It is attributed to the teller for their stay, and visible to those who manage the city.
* Each paid in gold under a false name: Eryndor as Johnny, Ryo as Leo (50 platinum), Kai as Rike, and Berberis as the Badger.
* Kai tried to read the imp's board and got dirt in his eye. Berberis's Insight found no shakedown: *"I'm just doing my job."*
* Below-deck refreshments were offered, *"although given your appearances and dispositions, I wouldn't recommend it."*
* Dondar's payment was left for a flashback scene next session, when Kiwi returns.
* The ferry cast off the moment the last foot touched the deck. A deeply hooded ferryman worked a long pole at the stern.

### Sahrim

* A tiger-headed figure in maroon and gold robes lounged on deck, purring with amusement: *"First time on the ferry, is it?"*
* The party deflected: they were *"here to see a guy about a dog."*
* *"You may call me [[Sahrim]]. I am the House of Provenance."* Information is meant to be traded back.
* He asked to see Ryo's ring. Ryo denied having one with a natural 20 on Deception, but failed a Wisdom save.
* **Major Beat:** *"No ring under the glove, eh? I see. You're very convincing… From Sumara, is it?"* Ryo held to *"from the Darkwood."*
* He eyed the [[Emberblade]]'s dwarf-faced hilt. Eryndor offered the [[Amulet of the Arachnid Queen]] to inspect instead.
* Sahrim: *"Not just a normal enchantment. None of these pathetic protection spells that those weakling wizards like to do from your realm."*
* Eryndor called it a gift from a friend, then failed a Wisdom save. *"Ah, this Nelfandriss, she's close to you then."*
* He wanted it for his collection. Eryndor will give his answer at Dis. *"House of Provenance, ask around. We're the most famous collector of antiquities in the city."*
* **Lore:** the House deals in collectibles: *"any thing, any item, any person."* Secrets are traded elsewhere in the city.
* He knew nothing of the red-robed mage. The chained spined devil had been left behind to be melted down.
* Kai's Insight (natural 19): he hadn't lied, he knows the city, and he would genuinely deal, but he means to take advantage of fresh faces.
* Kai noticed him rubbing the gold cuffs of his robe and told him to stop. *"Goddess, you say? … Good luck with that. You'll see."*

### A Picture of a Picture

* Berberis showed him the copy of the [[Red Priests]]' dragon scar, which drew a flash of hunger from him. Berberis wouldn't trade it.
* **Free Information:** Berberis: *"He's waking up and the worlds are shattering."*
* Berberis called Avernus a burnt, barren wasteland. Sahrim snarled, *"I'm allowed to call it that. You are not,"* then laughed it off.
* *"The rumors have been flying. Things are complicated. Me and mine, we don't get involved."* He has heard his master discuss it.
* **The Offer:** an introduction to the Master of the House of Provenance.
* *"Most mortals are foolish. They give up info too freely. They make deals lightly… You're up to something."*
* **Lore:** [[Riftshard|Riftshards]] are fewer in the Hells than on the Material Plane, perhaps because of their proximity to the initial shattering.
* Eryndor was uneasy at being called *"intriguing"*. Sahrim: *"Everything is an item in Dis. Just bear that in mind."*
* **Warning:** *"Be careful with that amulet. There's more to it than you know. There is a power inside of it that is dormant. It seems like it wishes to rear its head, given some trigger."*
* **Lore:** the House is the finest collection of relics in the Hells; people pay to visit its pieces, and its auctions are *"grander than you could imagine."*
* Six months ago, mortals brought it a 30-foot carved statue of a water elemental found floating in the Elemental Plane. No one knows who made it or how it got there.
* He returned to the prow to watch the river: *"The chained gates await us."*

### Below Deck

* Kai purified the provisions: pre-packaged balls of raw flesh, bone, teeth and hair. He ate a goodberry instead.
* A night hag sat munching on one, staring.
* A twitchy spined devil clutched a leather briefcase chained to his body.
* A knight in spiked black plate and a tattered red cloak, eyes faintly glowing yellow, read a book.
* Berberis's Medicine of 24: the mage had been hanging in a cage for a couple of weeks. A goodberry and water brought him round.
* *"Oh, how did I get out of that cage? I thought I was going to be there forever."* Berberis: *"Brother, you're in hell."*
* On souls: *"Oh, don't they all? Well, they can try, but I don't think they'll have much luck."*
* He showed no recognition of the party. *"Do me the honor of not asking me, and I won't ask you."*
* Berberis: *"Not often a voice ventures out into the world."* The mage: *"You're from the homeland, then."*
* He proposed sticking together for now. He had a place lined up, but is *"a little bit late."* Then he dozed off again.

### The Fallen Knight

* The knight spoke to Kai in a hollow voice about the moon on the [[Moonlit Aegis]]: *"That symbol, I recognize it… I used to wear one."*
* *"Why are you here? Did she send you for something? Did she send you for me?"*
* He slid his book away when Kai asked about it.
* Kai's shield sensed no [[Moonstone]] nearby; the last presence he felt was on the Material Plane. The knight had heard of the stones.
* He recoiled when Kai slid it closer: *"Please, do not make me look on it further. Please do not make me touch it."*
* He had forsaken her, *"a long time ago,"* and would say no more.
* Kai: *"She is kind and forgiving. If you find the urge, seek me."* Knight: *"I am beyond forgiveness. Do not play games with people like me down here, young one."*
* **Advice:** *"Cover that."* Kai later inked out the symbol with his illuminator's tattoo, and left three goodberries on the table, untouched.
* Berberis sat with his staff and a smile that said *"just try"* while Kai prayed. No one moved.
* Kai cast Guidance and prayed to Selûne about the knight. He felt only the faintest hint of confusion; reaching her here is hard.
* The ferryman ignored anyone who spoke to him.

### The Chain Gate

* The river was swallowed by mountains. Inside, the cavern walls faded until there was only the green lamp in a black void, time impossible to judge.
* A rust-coloured light grew ahead until the tunnel opened on the Chain Gate.
* Iron walls rose perhaps a thousand feet and ran out of sight. The chains hung from the top into the river, each link the size of the ferry.
* Ranks of flaming-winged figures patrolled the top. The chains rattled up, and erinyes descended.
* **The Manifest:** the gate captain read it aloud:
	* Sahrim, agent of the House of Provenance, by standing account.
	* Ser Corvane Ashmantle of the Dread Lancers, by legion's warrant.
	* Hesk of the Barrow-Ring, one soul.
	* Tiv, courier of the Third Talon, dispatches sealed.
	* One crate consigned to the Foundries, contents as stated.
	* Lucan Merrow of the Arcana Luminorium, passage paid in advance by arrangement.
	* Johnny, Leo, Rike and the Badger, each paid in gold.
* *"Declare any holy articles. Any business that is untoward the city will be dealt with harshly."*
* The erinyes landed and scanned each passenger.

### The Oath

* Kai declared his shield and two vials of holy water. The captain: *"Glad that we found honesty. A lot of our visitors are not such."*
* **Lore:** holy articles are confiscated unless a price is paid:
	* give a true name as surety
	* seal it under an iron oath, disabling it until you leave the city
	* swear an oath of non-use, on your soul
	* register it as a licensed holy artifact for a fee of 1,000 gold or a soul coin
	* have a patron vouch for it
* Sahrim, standing aside, gave Kai a wink.
* Ryo offered Kai his plain shield as a replacement.
* **Major Beat:** Kai signed an oath of non-use. The scroll forbids flaunting, intimidating with, or calling on any deity or patron through the holy artifact, or using it at all, until he leaves the city.
* Its enchantment means even blocking with it would break the oath. If he breaks it, his soul is forfeit.
* He signed as Rike, the name on the manifest. The captain's advice for next time: *"Just don't bring any holy artifacts."*
* *"Welcome to the city of Dis."* The gates opened wide, and Berberis had Kai stow the shield and holy water in the bag of holding to be safe.
* **Cliffhanger:** the ferry passes through the Chain Gate toward the Iron City, with the party in Dis under false names, Kai bound by a devil's contract, and Eryndor's coin secreted away.
