## Summary

The erinyes set a brutal pace across [[Avernus]], and when [[Kai]] asked if she was off to get herself killed again, she pointed to the far horizon, where roiling clouds were gathering. *"Soul storm. Come, we must be quick."* All afternoon she hustled the party into caves and behind outcrops while unseen hordes thundered past, until exhaustion forced a halt in a hidden cave, where Kai raised a darkened dome of force. [[Dondar]] tried to make friends and got a lesson in the Hells: questions and answers are currency, and nothing is given free. In trade for learning that the party had come to kill a patient devil, she told them she was an outrider of the Third Talon, a scouting company whose camp lay on the river, that her patrol had been ambushed and she alone survived, and that desertion is not looked on kindly. The only way to the city of Dis is down the Styx, she said, and passage must be bargained from a ferryman, whose manifest opens the Chain Gate. Asked what to call her, she warned them to be less careless with names, then gave the one her legion uses: [[Seraxis]].

That night [[Ryo]] turned his crystal ball on the devil they hunt. Through a crimson haze he found no body, only a formless rage with burning eyes and glowing horns, which turned toward him and screamed until the vision broke apart. Dondar turned a bag of over a thousand silver coins into fine silver dust for Kai, who spent the small hours making holy water. Then, near dawn, Dondar began thrashing in his sleep. The rune on his brow flared and a ball of fire burst against the top of the dome, scorching everyone inside. He woke screaming his sister's name. Shaken, he told them what he had never said before: when the regents took Glimmerforge for the Empire, his parents were sent to die in the mines. His brother's resistance had turned to suicide bombings, and his sister had been taken and sentenced to death deep underground. He had not heard from her since. He asked Kai to help him free the dwarves one day, and Kai promised to think about it. Seraxis swept in, warning that the blast may have given them away.

By morning the storm was only hours off, and between the party and the now-visible river two armies crashed together, demon horde against devil legion. Rather than lose hours going around, they chose to cut through the devils' back lines, and Seraxis drew her flaming sword, pleased: *"Good choice. Starting to like you all more."* Kai's whirling spirit blades scattered the dretches, Ryo's spectral wyverns guarded the flank, and [[Berberis]]'s owl, swollen into a great burning beast, carved a path. Dondar became a towering war elephant with Ryo loosing arrows from its back, even as fiery volleys, a hurled boulder, a diving vrock and a lashing chain came their way. Then Berberis woke a gnarled hell-tree, which tore the demon ranks apart and opened the gap that carried them through. At last they reached a spiked war camp on the riverbank, the camp of the Third Talon, with the storm less than an hour behind them. There Seraxis held out five chains. Collared as her property, she said, they would pass unquestioned. Berberis reminded her that her oath was to see them *to* the river, not onto it, and the party was left to decide whether to trust a devil and put on her chains.

## Details

### On the Erinyes' Heels

* Kai cast Aura of Vitality at 4th level after the gorge fight, healing 68 and topping everyone off.
* Kai: *"Slow down. You off to kill yourself again?"* Seraxis: *"Time doesn't wait here. We must take every opportunity we can."*
* **First Sight:** she pointed to roiling clouds on the far horizon: *"Soul storm. Come, we must be quick."*
* Kai: *"You sound afraid."* *"Not for myself. Only for you, with whom I have entered this pact. Perhaps foolishly, but I will see it to the end. You dying is not part of that."*
* Dondar had never heard of a soul storm. Riv placed it a dozen or more hours away, likely to hit the next day.
* Through the afternoon she pulled them into caves and behind outcrops as huge unseen hordes thundered past.
* As they tired, she offered the choice to rest or push on, warning that the stretch ahead would be harder. They chose to rest.

### The Cave

* Eryndor and Dondar searched for shelter. On a 26 investigation they found a mound of boulders hiding a hot cave about 20 feet across.
* Kai ritual-cast Leomund's Tiny Hut inside it, darkened so nothing could see in.
* Berberis sent his owl familiar up to scout, its usual blue glow now a fiendish red, with red eyes.
* **Vision of Hell:** wars in every direction, siege engines hurling house-sized boulders through the ranks, and endless screams and shrieks.
* On a 22 perception check, the owl spied a vast structure far off above a line of rocky crags, hidden in smoke and haze.
* Ryo climbed the rocks to keep lookout for 20 minutes. Nothing threatening was close.

### Questions Are Currency

* Seraxis: black plate veined with glowing embers, a full plated mask, burning horns and wings, a coiled rope at her waist, and a flaming greatsword on her shoulder.
* Dondar: *"Do you have any family?"* Seraxis: *"What game are you playing, dwarf?"*
* **Lore:** *"Questions and answers are valuable commodities here. One does not just give away information for free."* She asked why they had come in exchange.
* Dondar: *"We seek the death of a powerful one."* Neither he nor Eryndor could recall the name, so he settled for *"a very patient devil"*.
* *"Devils are patient. How else do you think we win over each other? We play the long game."* Killing one might help or hinder her, and she would rather not know.
* **Lore:** she is an outrider of the Third Talon, a scouting company that has been on tour on Avernus for a few dozen cycles. Its camp is by the river. She has no family and answers to her captain.
* Eryndor: *"So in a way, you're all alike?"* Her hand went to her sword and her eye-slits flared: *"Very clever, mortal."*
* She fights in the Blood War against the demons, *"the disgusting filth that spew out from the Abyss"*, and leaves the city's scheming to those with more time on their hands.
* **Lore:** there's no walking to Dis. The only way is down the Styx, the river that runs through every circle of the Hells, and only with permission.
* **Lore:** ferries and ferrymen each have a price. Strike a bargain and you're added to their cargo manifest, and the Chain Gate will open.
* *"If there's one thing that you can say about us, we hold the law highly above all things."*
* Her patrol was ambushed by demons on its way back, some already wounded. She was the only survivor and must return soon: *"Desertion is not looked upon kindly."*
* She doesn't sleep, and spent the night scouting and waiting impatiently.

### A Night in the Hut

* Kai shaped a five-foot ice cauldron with his decanter and prestidigitated the water to taste like the thick liquor from the [[Vale of Eternal Night]].
* Asked her name, she said: *"You're joking, aren't you?... Do not be so blasé with names here. A name is worth a lot."*
* **Major Beat:** *"You may address me as Seraxis. That is what my legion calls me. That is the name that I swear on."* She declined the drink: *"Drinking would be complicated for me."*
* She flatly refused to come into the hut.
* **Scrying the Devil:** hidden in the darkened hut, Ryo used his crystal ball to scry on Maloreth. The devil rolled a 2 on his save.
* The vision passed through warped red crystal into an angry, smoky haze. There was no body, just a formless shape with burning red eyes and glowing horns.
* **Major Beat:** the shape turned toward him and screamed, its smoky mouth rushing at the viewpoint, and the crystal ball went blank.
* Riv: a failed save means he won't know who scried on him, but creatures with truesight can see the sensor.
* **Reward Obtained:** Dondar cast Fabricate on Kai's 1,025 silver coins, which stacked, compressed and burst into 20 pounds of silver dust.
* After four hours' rest, Kai used Ceremony for two hours to make two vials of holy water, the silver glowing and fading into the water.
* Eryndor kept his vigil on watch, turning away a little under Seraxis's gaze.
* **Eryndor's Instinct:** an uneasy sense that she watches over them carefully, perhaps not only because of the bargain, but because she wants something from them.
* War came alarmingly close to the cave several times in the night.

### The Dream-Fire

* On the last watch, Kai saw Dondar muttering and thrashing, banging on the dome and kicking a sleeper.
* Kai's prestidigitation breeze didn't settle him.
* Dondar dreamed of dark tunnels lit by braziers, a distant explosion, a gust of wind, and angry footsteps coming toward him.
* **Major Beat:** the rune on his forehead glowed, and a streak of fire shot up and burst against the top of the dome.
* Everyone failed their saves and took 32 fire damage; Dondar took half. Kai absorbed elements.
* Dondar woke screaming *"Hvedra! Hvedra! No!"*, then collapsed.
* Riv ruled that the long rest still counted. Eryndor's Raven's Dagger didn't warn him, since Dondar meant no harm.
* **Character Moment:** Dondar told them about his family. His parents, kind and generous, raised him, his brother and his sister.
* When the regents took Glimmerforge for the Empire, his parents were sent to the mines to die.
* His brother, Hrothgar, formed a dwarven resistance, but has lost hope, and his methods have turned to suicide bombings.
* For this his sister was taken and sentenced to death deep underground. He has not heard from her.
* That is why he left to seek aid. He fears that the longer he's gone, the further his people and family slip into despair.
* *"So I had a dream. And I fought back. But the dream met life. Sorry."*
* He asked Kai to join him one day to free the dwarves. Kai: *"I'll think about it. If we survive, and we don't get fireballed again."*
* Seraxis came in: *"What was that sound? You may have alerted them to us. We need to get going."*
* Kai cast Aid at 5th level, then Aura of Vitality twice for 159 healing, bringing everyone to full.

### The Battle Line

* The storm was closer, only hours off. Seraxis led them through ravines until she halted them behind a stone column.
* Through the owl, Berberis watched two armies crash together on the plain: demon beasts, shambling brutes and siege engines against ordered devil ranks with erinyes among them.
* The river was now in sight, beyond the battlefield.
* Seraxis: go around and lose hours, letting the storm hit, or fight across. *"How much fun are you looking to have in your first romp through Avernus?"*
* Berberis asked if she could lead them through her own side's back lines. *"I can try. It won't be safe entirely."*
* Kai feared a tiny hut might not hold against the storm. They chose the battlefield.
* **Major Beat:** she drew a flame-licked sword: *"Good choice. I like what I'm hearing. Starting to like you all more… Well, I think this is gonna be fun."*

### Through the Ranks

* Riv ran the crossing as a run of ability checks, each failure bringing stray fire from the battlefield.
* Kai used Channel Divinity for 7 temporary hit points each, keeping Seraxis out of range and timing it to fade before the battlefield.
* Seraxis split a dretch up the middle and led them into the chaos among a regiment of barbed devils.
* **Success:** Kai cast Spirit Guardians at 5th level, a whirlwind of blades that cut the dretches while sparing the devils. In the confusion a barbed devil harpooned one to the ground.
* **Success:** Ryo cast Conjure Animals as spectral wyverns, like the elven guards' mounts in Sumara, and ran them along the line as a vanguard that knocked back a charging pack.
* Seraxis lassoed a gorilla-faced brute with her rope, pulled it down and ran it through without breaking stride.
* **Success:** Berberis channelled a 4th-level Summon Beast into his owl, a huge flaming owl that found and shouldered open a path.
* Kai's crow flew behind Seraxis's foes, belching the wolf howl it learned in the Vale.
* **Failure:** Eryndor lit the [[Emberblade]] with kindling to assassinate in Seraxis's wake, but a diving vrock tried to carry him off. He ducked and rolled clear.
* **Failure:** Dondar polymorphed into a giant war elephant, Tolkien-style, goring and trampling a path. A kneeling regiment loosed a flaming volley: 27 fire, half on a save. Eryndor evaded it, and Kai and Berberis absorbed elements.
* **Failure:** Ryo leapt onto Dondar's back to shoot from height. A hulking brute hurled a boulder at them both for 13 each, and Ryo lost concentration on his wyverns.
* **Failure:** Kai thorn-whipped foes into the elephant's path and lit them with starry wisp. A chain-wrapped devil lashed a chain at his legs, but he kicked free and whipped it back.
* **Major Beat:** Berberis had his familiar touch a gnarled, thorned hell-tree behind the demon lines and spent five staff charges to cast Awaken through it.
* The tree tore itself from the ground and hurled demons aside by the handful, like a Lord of the Rings ent, opening a gap. Berberis's staff has one charge left, and he refuses to ever spend it.
* Seraxis slipped in behind a pushing line of devils, and the party broke out the far side unpursued.

### The Camp of the Third Talon

* She drove them hard for the rest of the day, the storm perhaps an hour behind and the river about as far ahead.
* She stopped short of a large war camp built against the river, ringed by huge outward spikes, with sentries on its structures.
* It's a couple of hundred feet across and holds perhaps a thousand or two soldiers. They have very likely been spotted already.
* *"This is my camp. Camp of the Third Talon. There is a dock here that may allow you to board a ferry down the Styx, but getting you inside may be tricky."*
* **The Offer:** she unwound the chains she had gathered from the dead during the battle, five separate collars.
* *"If I place these around your necks, I can make it seem like you are my possessions, and we will not be questioned. I found you on the battlefield. Vagrant souls who made a deal with me to allow them passage, and in return, I would have their souls."*
* *"It is up to you, though. I cannot force you to put them on."*
* Berberis: *"The promise was to see us to the river, not onto the river."* Ryo and Berberis made insight checks, and Riv sent the results privately.
* Asked what happens once they're on the river: she'd have to move quickly, since letting them go openly would raise eyebrows.
* **Lore:** a bargain with the ferryman is binding. Once they're on the river no one will chase them, because they'd have to take it up with the ferryman.
* How they get onto the ferry is not her concern: *"That was not part of our agreement. My agreement was to get you to the river."* Kai: *"We're not at the river yet."* *"Exactly."*
* **Cliffhanger:** with the storm closing and a garrison they can't fight their way through, the party must decide whether to take Seraxis at her word and wear her chains, or find another way.
