The Nine Hells are a plane of nine descending layers, each more dangerous to mortals than the one above it, ruled by devils and bound together by law, contract and the trade in souls. To scholars of [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]] they are a place known almost entirely from old accounts and fragments: a realm where gold is welcome, souls are minted into coin, and a careless word can be worth more to its hearer than a sword. Every traveller who enters arrives first in [[Avernus]], the war-torn uppermost layer, and must find their own way down.

## Nature of the Plane

The Hells are lawful to their core. Their devils are evil, but they keep to rules, contracts and hierarchies, and they would rather bind a mortal in a bad bargain than kill one outright. The demons that war against them across the first layer keep to nothing at all, and will tear a mortal apart simply for being mortal.

Death works differently here. A devil slain in the mortal world does not truly die; its essence returns to the Hells and reforms there in time. A devil slain within the Hells is destroyed for good. That is why a devil's enemies, if they mean to end it, must follow it home.

Names carry weight. A true name freely given does not bind its owner, but it grants the hearer power over them and makes them known, and in the Hells to be known is a danger of its own. Travellers are advised to give their names sparingly, and never for free.

The heat of the upper layers is oppressive but not deadly: a mortal sweats the moment they arrive, and a long march wears them down, but the ground does not burn them where they stand. Clean water and food are scarce, and travellers are wise to bring their own.

The light of [[Selûne]]'s faithful is unwelcome here. When healing moonlight was turned on a devil in Avernus, it mended her wounds but hissed and burned on her skin as it did so, while healing drawn from the living land gave her no pain at all.

## Geography & Landscape

The plane is built as a descent. Its nine layers sit one beneath another, and the deeper a traveller goes, the worse the layer becomes. Only the first two are known in any detail.

[[Avernus]], the first layer, is a rust-red wasteland under a burning sky, an endless battlefield of the Blood War littered with the wreckage of armies. Below it lies [[Dis]], the second layer, which is not so much a city on its layer as a city that is the layer: the Iron City, a single sprawl of iron reaching across the whole of it.

Through every layer runs the river Styx, the fastest road across Avernus and the way down. Ferries work its waters, and those who know the Hells give one piece of advice above all others: never enter the water.

## History

Little of the Hells' history is known in Elandis. During the [[Chaos Wars]], some two and a half thousand years ago, the devils took no direct part; the conflict was confined mainly to the material world, and they were only ancillary to it, profiting from the souls and bargains of those desperate for power.

Long ago, [[Mirileth]] and the young [[Ellesandra Silmara]] hunted down the archdevil [[Maloreth the Patient]], destroyed his body, and sealed his soul inside the [[Soulstone Anchor]], the one prison from which an archdevil cannot simply reform in the Hells.

## Peoples & Powers

Devils rule the Hells. They are lawful and patient, and the senior among them are said to speak Common. In the deeper layers they are less inclined to kill mortals who have business there than to trap them in bargains, read exactly as written.

Demons are the devils' eternal enemies. Their endless war with the devils, the Blood War, is fought across Avernus without pause. Sumara's archives record mindless swarms in Avernus, dretches and lemures among them, that attack anything in their path, and vulture-like beasts that snatch prey from the ground to drop it from a height. Travellers there have since met the vrocks, vulture-headed demons whose screech can stun a mortal where they stand, and the hezrou, a hulking, foul-smelling brute whose stench sickens all around it.

The erinyes are among the devils' soldiers: tall, armoured, winged warriors who fight with great swords and glowing ropes that can bind a foe or drag a flier from the sky.

## Entry & Egress

There is no easy road into the Hells, and an easier road in than out. Travellers who lack great power, or ties to one of the deeper layers, always arrive in [[Avernus]] and must fight or bargain their way down from there. Magic that carries a traveller between planes cannot be aimed past that first layer, and even the old [[Silmara Family|Silmara]], for all their knowledge, could never open a [[World Gates|World Gate]] any deeper than Avernus.

A World Gate opened onto the Hells always lands in the same place in Avernus, and closes within moments of the travellers stepping through. No gate stands waiting on the far side. Messages sent by magic can still cross between the Hells and the material world.

From Avernus, the way down to Dis is the Styx and the ferries that run on it. Passage must be paid for.

## Realms & Territories

- [[Avernus]], the first layer: a burning wasteland and the battlefield of the Blood War, where every traveller arrives.
- [[Dis]], the second layer: the Iron City, a single city spanning the entire layer.

## Dangers

- Bargains: everything is offered as a deal, and every deal is binding.
- Names: a true name given away is power handed to its hearer.
- Demons: in Avernus they attack mortals on sight, in packs and swarms.
- The Styx: its waters are not to be entered.
- The heat: long marches through the upper layers wear travellers down, and clean food and water are hard to come by.

## Economy

Material gold spends in the Hells, and devils welcome it. The plane's own currency is the soul coin, a coin holding a damned soul inside it. Breaking a soul coin frees the soul within, usually to be devoured. Those who have carried soul coins say that, held close, they can almost be heard.

## [[The Bloody Nails|Campaign: The Bloody Nails]]

#### [[Session 52 - The Star-Veiled Queen]]

[[Mirileth]] named the Hells as the final stage of her price for the [[Soulstone Anchor]]: once the stone was broken, the party must travel there and destroy the reforming body of the archdevil [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]] before he regained his strength.

#### [[Session 56 - Homecomings and Farewells]]

[[Ryo]] confessed the bargain to [[Ayana Syndrosa|Ayana]]: they must go to the Nine Hells and kill someone. Ayana revealed that the [[World Gates|World Gate]] at the base of the [[Heartstone]], driven by Ryo alone, could take them there, and the party resolved to research the Hells before going.

#### [[Session 57 - On the Eve of Hell]]

In the [[Lectorium]], the party learned the shape of the Hells: nine layers, every traveller arriving in [[Avernus]], Dis spanning the whole second layer, the Styx as the road down, devils who bargain and demons who kill. They also learned that gold spends there, souls are minted into coin, and names should be given sparingly. Buried in an account of a hideous play staged for great devils, they found [[Maloreth the Patient|Maloreth]]'s name beside a theatre in the Rustward quarter of Dis.

#### [[Session 58 - Into Avernus]]

Warned by [[Belinda Bubblegum|Belinda]] to guard their names and carry water and fire-wards, the party passed through the World Gate beneath the Heartstone into [[Avernus]]. There they fought beside a wounded erinyes against a pack of demons, and she swore to see them to the river.

#### [[Session 59 - Across the Waste]]

On the march across [[Avernus]], the erinyes [[Seraxis]] taught the party how the Hells work. Questions and answers are currency, never given away free, and names are worth a great deal. There is no walking to Dis. The only road is the Styx, the river that runs through every circle of the Hells, and travel on it needs permission. Ferries and ferrymen each set a price, and a traveller who strikes a bargain is added to the cargo manifest, which opens the Chain Gate. A bargain with a ferryman is binding, so no one will chase a passenger once they're on the river. As she put it, *"we hold the law highly above all things."*

#### [[Session 60 - Down the Styx]]

The party took passage down the Styx from the Third Talon's dock. An imp clerk set the fare at 500 gold, a soul coin, a memory, a true story the ferryman chooses, or a true name. Every passenger must have a name on the register, so the party paid in gold under false names. The ferry carried a tiger-headed agent of the House of Provenance, a night hag, a devil courier, a fallen knight, and a caged imperial mage. Its provisions were balls of raw flesh and bone. The river vanished into the mountains, through a black void where time could not be judged, and came out at the Chain Gate. Its iron walls rise perhaps a thousand feet, and each link of its chains is the size of the ferry. An erinyes captain read the manifest aloud and demanded that holy articles be declared. They could be confiscated, or kept under a true-name surety, an iron seal, an oath of non-use, a paid licence, or a patron's word. [[Kai]] swore non-use of his shield and holy water, and the ferry passed on into Dis.
