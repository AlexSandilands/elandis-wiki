Avernus is the first and uppermost layer of the [[Nine Hells]]: a rust-red wasteland under a burning sky, and the battlefield of the Blood War, the eternal conflict between devils and demons. Every traveller who enters the Hells, by spell or by gate, arrives here first. It is not a destination but a threshold, and the only safe way through it is to find the river Styx and the passage down to Dis.

## Geography & Landscape

Avernus forms the top of the Hells' great descent, with [[Dis]], the Iron City, lying below it. Its extent is unknown; to those standing in it, the waste simply runs to every horizon.

The ground is cracked, red and hot, broken by ridges, gorges and steep scrambles of rock. Pools of lava bubble in the low places, and fumes rise from them to fill the gorges with drifting smoke. What little grows there is withered and gnarled: brambles and the husks of dead trees. Gusts of hot wind come and go beneath a sky of roiling cloud.

The whole plain is a graveyard of war. Crushed and broken siege engines lie everywhere, among them trebuchets whose buckets are the size of houses and whose wheels tower over anyone passing beneath, some still smouldering with embers. The wreckage is the remnant of a war that began before any mortal could remember, and it is still being fought. Overhead, winged creatures tear at one another; across the plain, pockets of dust mark skirmishes, and whole armies clash on the horizon. The air carries a constant hum of distant screams, shouts and the clash of blades.

The river Styx cuts across the waste. From the ridge where the [[World Gates|World Gate]] opens, it can be seen far off as a cut in the land at the foot of a distant line of mountains.

## Nature of the Layer

Arrival in Avernus is a shock of heat: the temperature leaps from mild to very hot, and sweat runs at once, though the air does not burn. It is uncomfortable rather than deadly, but a long march wears travellers down, and they are wise to bring water and to keep cool where they can.

Travellers of faith may feel it more keenly still. A priest of [[Selûne]] arriving there felt a sudden distance from his goddess, as if cut off.

## Peoples & Powers

Avernus belongs to no one. Devils and demons fight over it endlessly, and neither side spares much regard for mortals caught between them.

Demons hunt in packs and swarms and attack mortals on sight; to them a mortal soul is the choicest prey in the layer. Those met here include:

- Dretches: ratlike, pustule-covered wretches that swarm in clambering heaps, snapping and clawing.
- Vrocks: vulture-headed demons that dive out of the smoke. Their screech can stun a mortal where they stand, and they seize prey in their claws to carry it into the sky.
- Hezrou: hulking, toadlike brutes with massive claws, wreathed in a stench that sickens anyone near them.

Devils hold the other side of the line. The erinyes are among their soldiers: tall, armoured, faceless behind full-plate helms, with blazing horns, wings of fire, great swords and glowing ropes that bind foes and drag fliers from the sky. They bargain and swear oaths, and they keep them exactly.

## Entry & Egress

Every road into the Hells lands in Avernus. A World Gate opened onto the Hells always arrives at the same place, high on a ridge above the waste, and seals within seconds of the travellers stepping through, with no gate waiting on the far side.

The way onward is the Styx, the fastest route across the layer and the road down to Dis. Ferries work its waters for a price. The water itself must never be entered.

## Dangers

- The Blood War: skirmishes flare across the plain without warning, and mortals caught in them are prey for both sides.
- Demon packs: dretch swarms, vrocks diving from the smoke, and hezrou running down whatever they chase.
- Lava and fumes: molten pools in the low ground and choking smoke in the gorges.
- The heat: draining over any long journey, with little clean food or water to be found.
- The Styx: its water is never to be touched.

## Significance

Avernus is the threshold of the Hells. No one reaches the deeper layers without first crossing it and earning, or buying, the way down.

## [[The Bloody Nails|Campaign: The Bloody Nails]]

#### [[Session 58 - Into Avernus]]

[[Ryo]] opened the World Gate beneath the [[Heartstone]] and the party stepped through onto a ridge above the burning waste. [[Kai]] spotted the river at the foot of distant mountains, and the party descended by rope and marched past smouldering siege engines and lava pools into a fuming gorge. There a wounded erinyes, fleeing a hezrou, swarms of dretches and a flight of vrocks, called on them to stand with her in exchange for an escort to the river. They fought beside her: [[Dondar]]'s conjured hand drove a vrock down into a lava pool, [[Eryndor]] killed the hezrou, and the erinyes cut the last vrock apart once [[Berberis]] had bound it to the ground. Furious that Kai's holy healing had burned her, she warned him never to try it again, then kept her oath and set off for the Styx with the party in tow.

#### [[Session 59 - Across the Waste]]

The party followed the erinyes [[Seraxis]] across the waste toward the Styx, racing a soul storm that was rolling in from the horizon. She hid them behind outcrops as hordes thundered past, and they spent the night in a hidden cave while the war raged close by. From the air, [[Berberis]]'s owl saw battles in every direction, siege engines hurling house-sized boulders through the ranks, and a vast structure far off above a line of crags, hidden in smoke. The next morning a demon horde and a devil legion clashed between the party and the river, and rather than lose hours going around, they cut through the devils' back lines. Spirit blades, spectral wyverns, a burning owl, [[Dondar]] as a war elephant and an awakened hell-tree carried them through. They reached the river at the spiked camp of the Third Talon, Seraxis's company, with the storm less than an hour behind them.

## Trivia

- The erinyes who met the party swore her oath *"on my name and my wing"* before they knew her name. When she finally gave it, as [[Seraxis]], it came with a warning to be less careless with names in the Hells.
