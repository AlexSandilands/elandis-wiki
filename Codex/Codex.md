The Codex is the in-world encyclopaedia of [[Elandis]] — one page for every person, place, thing, power and event worth remembering. It is the player-facing record of the world as the table has come to know it, gathered into five great categories.

Use this page as the index to all of them. Each section below opens with what you will find there, then lists every entry currently published, grouped by kind.

> [!info] A living, partial record
> This wiki is only a subset of the full archive, so as not to spoil what the table hasn't yet uncovered. You will sometimes meet "dead" links — references to pages held back behind the curtain. They will surface here as the story reveals them.

> [!tip] Looking for the campaign?
> The Codex is the world; the play records live with [[The Bloody Nails]]. Start at the [[Elandis Wiki|wiki home]] to see both.
