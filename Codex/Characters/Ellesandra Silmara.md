**Ellesandra Silmara** is a legendary ancestor of the [[Silmara Family]] who ruled [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]] during the conflict known as the [[Vale of Eternal Night#The Long Hunt *(~2000 years ago)*|Long Hunt]] — a sustained campaign by the riding-bands of the [[Vale of Eternal Night|Vale]] against the elves of the Shimmering Peaks. She is remembered above all else for a single act: walking alone into the Vale and returning with the head of the leader of the Hunt.

## Biography

The Long Hunt ground on for years, devastating settlements on both sides of the boundary between the Vale and Sumaran territory. Ellesandra ruled through it, bearing the weight of a war that showed no sign of ending by negotiation or force of arms. At some point, she made a decision that no record has ever fully explained.

She walked into the [[Vale of Eternal Night]] alone.

She came back carrying the head of the leader of the Hunt. What passed inside the Vale — what she saw, who she spoke with, what she gave or promised or did to emerge again — was never spoken of. The war ended. Soon after her return, the archfae [[Mirileth|Mirileth, the Star-Veiled Queen]] rose to take the throne of the Court, and a tenuous peace settled over the border that has held for two thousand years since.

Ellesandra made a pact with the archfae queen to end the conflict: the queen would take control of the Vale, and the war would cease. The terms of that pact, if any were written down, are lost.

Two thousand years later, Ellesandra stepped briefly out of that shared form. After much convincing, Mirileth granted her a short span of time apart, as part of a bargain struck with [[Ryo]] and his companions. She spent it in the guise of a small, dark-haired elven girl who called herself Ellessa, an orphan of Sumara taken into the darkness. Through all of it she was Ellesandra alone: the Queen was never in the child. She used the time to return to the [[Vault of Memories]] and see [[Praetor'Varanous]], her old friend, who had felt her go into the Vale and waited two thousand years for her to come back out. Then she went back to her vigil, holding the darkness of the Vale in check for as long as she must.

## [[The Bloody Nails|Campaign: The Bloody Nails]]

#### [[Session 50 - A New Morning in Sumara]]

[[Warden Sylvaris]] told [[Ryo]] and the party the story of the Long Hunt beneath the bioluminescent fields of the Greensward, naming Ellesandra as an ancestor of Ryo's line and recounting her solitary journey into the Vale — and her return with the Hunt-leader's head. The account was delivered as received history: what she did is documented; how she did it is not.

#### [[Session 52 - The Star-Veiled Queen]]

The truth of Ellesandra's walk into the Vale, untold for two thousand years, was finally spoken — by the one who had been there. When [[Mirileth|the Star-Veiled Queen]] stayed her hand from killing [[Ryo]] and recognised the [[Silmara Family|Silmara]] blood in him, she revealed that she and Ellesandra are one. The pact that ended the Long Hunt was a trade of flesh: Ellesandra surrendered her body for the archfae to wear, in exchange for the toppling of the Hunt and the seizing of the Vale. Her recorded death in [[Sumara, The Shining City|Sumara]] years later was in truth her return to the Vale to give up that body, and the two minds have shared one form ever since. In moments of strain a flicker of Ellesandra still surfaces behind the Queen's eyes — enough, the party sensed, to have stirred her mercy toward her own descendant.

#### [[Session 58 - Into Avernus]]

The girl Ellessa, who had travelled with the party since they left the Vale, led them down through the [[Vault of Memories]] by its fastest road, explaining that the vault had last accepted her about two thousand years ago. In the dragon's hall she ran to [[Praetor'Varanous|Praetor]] with a present, a stolen potion that left him hiccupping snow. When he named her, the child unfurled into a tall elven woman with long silver hair. *"You got old."* *"I was always old. You got small."* She wept in his arms, then thanked the party and declared the bargain complete, promising to release the devil whenever they were ready and to send the stone once they returned and sent word. She told them the Queen had never been with them: *"It was just me."* The time had been granted in exchange for the deal, and it was only temporary. To [[Ryo]] she apologised for watching him, said she saw a lot of him in her son, and told him the weight of thousands of years did not have to carry its expectations: *"It's still your free choice."* She sat and traded old jokes with Praetor until she faded on a wind and was gone. Praetor then told the party her story as Sumara remembers it: a hero assumed to have sacrificed herself to end the war, who in truth has spent two thousand years holding the darkness at bay.
