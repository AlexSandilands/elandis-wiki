Sahrim is a tiger-headed denizen of [[Dis]] and an agent of the House of Provenance, which he calls the finest collection of relics and curiosities in all the [[Nine Hells]], perhaps in all the planes. Courteous, amused and quietly menacing, he rides the ferries of the Styx looking for things worth collecting.

## Biography

Sahrim has the head of a tiger, with two large fangs in an easy smile and a low chuckle that is almost a purr. He dresses in rich maroon and gold robes and elegant boots, and he lounges as though every barge had been waiting for him. He has a habit of rubbing the gold cuffs of his sleeves. He introduces himself as being of the House of Provenance, and he calls it the most famous collector of antiquities in the city, which in Dis is no small thing. He claims to remember everything he sees as part of his job.

The House deals in collectibles, *"any thing, any item, any person."* It leaves the trade in secrets to others, though a secret of particular importance can still interest it. Visitors pay to see its pieces, it trades away what it tires of for something more intriguing, and its auctions are said to be grander than anyone could imagine. Sahrim serves a master he calls only the Master of the House of Provenance.

He treats every conversation as a trade. He gives a small truth freely to buy goodwill and expects information in return. He keeps out of the politics of the Hells himself, and holds that in Dis everything is an item.

## [[The Bloody Nails|Campaign: The Bloody Nails]]

#### [[Session 60 - Down the Styx]]

- On the ferry from the Third Talon camp, he quizzed the party about why mortals would come to Dis willingly. When [[Ryo]] denied having a ring, Sahrim saw through him: *"From Sumara, is it?"*
- He plucked the name of [[Nelfandriss the Banished]] from [[Eryndor]]'s thoughts and coveted the [[Amulet of the Arachnid Queen]]. He warned Eryndor that a dormant power inside it waits for some trigger, and left an offer to buy it open until they reach Dis.
- When [[Berberis]] showed him the copied dragon of the [[Red Priests]] and said *"he's waking up and the worlds are shattering,"* Sahrim offered an introduction to the Master of the House of Provenance.
- [[Kai]] noticed him rubbing his cuffs and told him to stop. Hearing that Kai serves a goddess, Sahrim said only *"Good luck with that. You'll see."* At the Chain Gate he winked at Kai when the captain mentioned a patron vouching for his holy articles.
