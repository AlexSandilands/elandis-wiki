Seraxis is an erinyes of the [[Nine Hells]], an outrider of the Third Talon, a scouting company of devils serving its tour on the Blood War front in [[Avernus]]. Curt, contemptuous and bound to the letter of her word, she has no interest in the politics of the cities below, preferring, in her own words, blood and steel.

## Biography

Seraxis stands tall in black plate veined with glowing embers, her face hidden behind a plated mask and crowned with burning horns. Her wings are made of fire. She fights with a flame-licked greatsword and a glowing rope she uses to lasso and drag down her enemies, and she cuts through demons without breaking stride. She does not sleep, declines food and drink, and keeps watch through the night with barely concealed impatience.

She has no family and answers to her captain. She holds the Blood War to be the only honest work, calls demons *"the disgusting filth that spew out from the Abyss"*, and leaves the scheming of Dis to those who think themselves cleverer than the rest, who she says only have more time on their hands. Like all her kind she holds the law above all things, and she treats knowledge as currency: one does not give away information for free. Seraxis is the name her legion calls her, and she gave it with a warning to be less careless with names in the Hells.

After a demon pack ambushed her patrol as it returned from the front, she was the only survivor. With every hour away from her company she risks being taken for a deserter, and desertion is not looked on kindly.

## [[The Bloody Nails|Campaign: The Bloody Nails]]

#### [[Session 58 - Into Avernus]]

Wounded and fleeing a hezrou, dretch swarms and vrocks, she called on the party to stand with her, swearing *"on my name and my wing"* to see them to the river. She fought beside them and cut the last vrock apart, but [[Kai]]'s holy healing burned her each time, and she warned him: *"If we had not made a bargain, I would slay you where you stand."* Then she kept her oath and set off for the Styx with the party behind her.

#### [[Session 59 - Across the Waste]]

She led the party across [[Avernus]] ahead of a soul storm, hiding them from passing hordes, and taught them the ways of the Hells. She traded [[Dondar]] her own history for the reason they had come, explained that the only road to Dis is the Styx, by a ferryman's leave, and gave the name her legion uses. After Dondar's sleeping fireball she hurried them on. Faced with a battle between them and the river, she approved of their choice to cut through her own side's back lines and led them across, sword drawn: *"Starting to like you all more."* At the Third Talon's camp she offered to collar them in chains and lead them in as her property, reminding them that her oath ran only as far as the river.
